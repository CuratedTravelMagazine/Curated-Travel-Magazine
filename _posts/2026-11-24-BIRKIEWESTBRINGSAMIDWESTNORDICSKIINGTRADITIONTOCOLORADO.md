<figure><img alt="" src="https://substack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com/public/images/c0c12d22-7f31-4f3e-853c-ba8940725c17_3024x2168.jpeg"/></figure><p>The American Birkebeiner Ski Foundation is bringing its celebrated Nordic skiing tradition west with Birkie West, a new event at YMCA of the Rockies’ Snow Mountain Ranch in Granby, Colorado. Drawing on the legacy of the Wisconsin Birkie, the weekend will bring together recreational skiers, competitive athletes and the wider Nordic community, with SNØ Racing athletes participating in races, cheering on competitors and leading clinics. Organizers expect more than 500 racers and 750 spectators. Set across Snow Mountain Ranch’s 5,100 acres, the destination offers more than 80 kilometers of groomed Nordic trails, making the event an opportunity to experience Colorado’s mountain landscape through one of winter’s most enduring outdoor sports.</p><figure><img alt="" src="https://substack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com/public/images/0fa81f76-8bad-4f8c-b4ac-39ae46a44732_1200x500.jpeg"/><figcaption>Photo Credit: Courtesy of YMCA of the Rockies Snow Mountain Ranch.</figcaption></figure><p><strong>The Curated Take</strong></p><p>Birkie West reflects the growing appeal of destination-based outdoor events that bring together competition, community and access to the natural landscape. For travelers, it offers another reason to explore Colorado beyond its downhill ski resorts, while introducing the camaraderie and traditions of Nordic skiing to a broader audience. The combination of a major sporting weekend, extensive trail network and mountain lodging makes Snow Mountain Ranch an appealing winter destination for both dedicated skiers and those curious about the sport.</p>
