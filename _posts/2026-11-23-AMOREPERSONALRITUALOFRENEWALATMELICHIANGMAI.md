<figure><img alt="" src="https://substack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com/public/images/770e8805-2f72-4642-95e8-07cfa8f6a5fc_960x640.jpeg"/></figure><p>At Meliá Chiang Mai, wellness takes a customizable approach at YHI Spa, where a new Wellness Spa Buffet invites guests to create a treatment experience shaped around their individual needs. The concept brings together seven therapies, allowing visitors to combine traditional Thai massage, aromatic and deep tissue treatments with foot massage, body scrubs, herbal compresses, and aloe vera wraps in a two-hour spa experience. Rooted in Asian healing traditions and complemented by contemporary wellness techniques, the spa’s broader menu also includes a signature treatment that pairs Northern Thai Tok Sen techniques with Spanish massage. Seven private treatment rooms, designed around an underwater theme, provide a tranquil setting, complemented by a Thai herbal steam and sauna. Located in the heart of Chiang Mai, the spa offers travelers an opportunity to step away from the pace of exploring northern Thailand and experience a more restorative side of the city.</p><figure><img alt="" src="https://substack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com/public/images/0fa81f76-8bad-4f8c-b4ac-39ae46a44732_1200x500.jpeg"/><figcaption>Photo Credit: Chiang Mai, Thailand. Courtesy of Meliá Chiang Mai.</figcaption></figure><h3>The Curated Take</h3><p>Wellness does not always require an elaborate retreat or a complete departure from travel. Sometimes, the extraordinary is found in the opportunity to pause and choose what the body needs in that moment. Meliá Chiang Mai’s treatment concept brings an element of personal discovery to the spa experience, while its incorporation of Northern Thai healing traditions offers a connection to regional wellness practices. It is a thoughtful counterpoint to the movement and exploration that often define a journey through Chiang Mai.</p>
