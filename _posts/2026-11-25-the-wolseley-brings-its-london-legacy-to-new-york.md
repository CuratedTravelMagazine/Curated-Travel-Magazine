A storied New York clubhouse is preparing for a new chapter, this time as the first hotel from The Wolseley Hotels. Set inside the McKim, Mead & White-designed former Lambs Club on West 44th Street, The Wolseley Hotel New York will bring 77 guest rooms and suites to Midtown, alongside a reimagined wellness center and the brand’s first restaurant in the United States. The Wolseley Members Club will add another dimension to the address, with a private, all-day setting for dining, culture and conversation.

**The Curated Take**

New York has no shortage of luxury hotels, but a property with a historic gathering-place identity and an established restaurant heritage brings a different proposition. The combination of hotel, dining and private club could make this more than somewhere to stay between city engagements.
