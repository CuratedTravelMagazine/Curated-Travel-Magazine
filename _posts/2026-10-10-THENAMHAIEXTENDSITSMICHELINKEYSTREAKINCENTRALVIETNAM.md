<p>Four Seasons Resort The Nam Hai in Hoi An has retained its two MICHELIN Keys in the 2026 MICHELIN Guide hotel selection, remaining the only two-Key resort in Danang for a second consecutive year. The recognition arrives as the beachfront resort approaches its 10th anniversary under the Four Seasons banner, offering a useful lens on what has sustained its reputation: personalized service, Vietnamese cultural connections, wellness and a strong sense of place along the central coast.</p>
<p>The milestone year also brings a refreshed accommodation offering, with the resort recently completing a renovation of its Three-Bedroom Signature Pool Villas. The redesigned villas pair contemporary comforts with the property’s established architectural character, while experiences range from treatments at The Heart of The Earth Spa to cultural activities rooted in Central Vietnam. Dining and wellness programming round out a stay that is designed to connect guests not only with the resort, but with the landscape and traditions surrounding Hoi An.</p>
<p><strong>The Curated Take:</strong> The Nam Hai’s latest MICHELIN Key recognition is less interesting as an award than as evidence of staying power. Nearly a decade into its Four Seasons chapter, the resort continues to build its luxury proposition around Vietnam itself rather than simply placing a luxury hotel on a beautiful beach. That combination of service, architecture, wellness and cultural connection gives the resort a sense of continuity at a time when luxury hospitality increasingly competes on novelty.</p>
