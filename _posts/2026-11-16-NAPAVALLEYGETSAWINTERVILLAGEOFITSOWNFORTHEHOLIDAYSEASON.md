Celebrating its 20th anniversary, the resort is introducing new experiences alongside returning seasonal traditions, including a holiday pop-up bar, interactive children’s activities, a weekend market featuring local vendors and expanded Princess Tinsel Teas. Guests can skate at Napa Valley’s largest outdoor ice rink, gather around fire pits for s’mores, visit Santa’s Workshop or enjoy sparkling high tea. New glass rinkside cabins offer a private setting for groups to take in the festivities, while nightly tree lighting and snowfall create a festive atmosphere across the property. From family-friendly celebrations to seasonal cocktails and intimate gatherings, Merry Meritage brings a winter holiday experience to California’s renowned wine region.</p><figure><img alt="" src="https://substack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com/public/images/0fa81f76-8bad-4f8c-b4ac-39ae46a44732_1200x500.jpeg"/><figcaption>Photo Credit: Napa Valley, California. Courtesy of The Meritage Resort &amp; Spa.</figcaption></figure><h3>The Curated Take</h3><p>There is something particularly appealing about a holiday escape that places seasonal traditions in an unexpected setting. Napa Valley is more often associated with vineyard landscapes, wine tasting and leisurely weekends, but Merry Meritage introduces another side of the destination, one shaped by winter festivities, culinary indulgence and the pleasure of gathering. The resort’s blend of outdoor skating, intimate fire pits, festive dining and local shopping gives the experience a sense of occasion without losing its Wine Country identity. For travelers looking to trade a traditional snowy getaway for a California holiday retreat, it offers a different kind of seasonal atmosphere.</p>
