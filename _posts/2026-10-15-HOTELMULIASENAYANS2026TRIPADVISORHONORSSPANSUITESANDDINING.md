<p>Hotel Mulia Senayan in Jakarta has earned multiple Tripadvisor Travelers’ Choice recognitions for 2026, extending across its accommodation and dining experiences. The Suites at Hotel Mulia Senayan received Travelers’ Choice Best of the Best, placing it among the top 1% of properties on Tripadvisor, while the hotel itself ranks among the top 10% of hotels on the platform. The recognition also reaches into the hotel’s culinary portfolio, with Table8 among the top 1% of restaurants worldwide and Edogin, Cascade Lounge, il Mare and The Café among the top 10%.</p>
<p>The breadth of the honors offers a snapshot of what has made Hotel Mulia Senayan a longstanding presence in Jakarta’s luxury hospitality scene. Located in the Senayan district near the Jakarta Convention Center and the city’s only golf course within the city center, the 994-room hotel combines extensive accommodations and event spaces with a notably broad dining program. The Suites, introduced in 2015, add a more personalized layer of hospitality through dedicated butler service and individually tailored details, while the property’s international art collection extends throughout guest rooms and public spaces.</p>
<p><strong>The Curated Take:</strong> Rather than recognizing a single standout experience, Tripadvisor’s 2026 honors touch nearly every part of the Hotel Mulia Senayan experience, from its most private accommodations to its restaurants and lounge. For travelers looking at Jakarta through the lens of established luxury, that consistency across such a large property is part of the story.</p>
