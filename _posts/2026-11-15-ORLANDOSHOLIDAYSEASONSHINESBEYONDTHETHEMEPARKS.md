Across the destination, Universal Orlando Resort is extending its seasonal celebrations into the new Epic Universe, while LEGOLAND Florida introduces a Christmas drone show featuring LEGO Santa. Returning highlights include Walt Disney World’s holiday festivities and Dazzling Nights at Harry P. Leu Gardens, where illuminated landscapes and new performance elements create an immersive evening experience. Beyond the major attractions, downtown Orlando will host a free Frontyard Holiday Festival with nightly snowfall, live entertainment and a European-inspired holiday market, while ette hotel is partnering with Orlando Ballet for a Nutcracker-themed afternoon tea. At Evermore Orlando Resort, Grande Lakes, Four Seasons Resort Orlando and Conrad Orlando, seasonal programming adds another dimension to the destination’s holiday appeal. From family celebrations to sophisticated dining and festive resort escapes, Orlando offers a wide-ranging approach to winter travel in a destination known for its warm-weather setting.</p><figure><img alt="" src="https://substack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com/public/images/0fa81f76-8bad-4f8c-b4ac-39ae46a44732_1200x500.jpeg"/><figcaption>Photo Credit: Orlando, Florida. Courtesy of Visit Orlando.</figcaption></figure><h3>The Curated Take</h3><p>Orlando’s holiday appeal extends well beyond its reputation as a theme park destination. The breadth of seasonal programming reveals a more layered holiday escape, where immersive entertainment can be balanced with cultural performances, illuminated gardens, distinctive dining and resort experiences. For travelers seeking a winter celebration without the cold-weather setting, the destination offers considerable variety, whether the occasion calls for a multigenerational family gathering, a festive weekend away or a longer holiday stay. The most interesting additions are those that bring a sense of place to the season, from the Nutcracker-inspired tea to the downtown carillon and neighborhood celebrations.</p>
