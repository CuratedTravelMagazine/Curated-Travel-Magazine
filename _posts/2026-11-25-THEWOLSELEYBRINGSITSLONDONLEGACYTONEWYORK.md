<figure><img alt="" src="https://substack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com/public/images/e02f29c0-f020-4975-820c-f6caf62f3792_2500x1907.jpeg"/></figure><p>A storied New York clubhouse is preparing for a new chapter, this time as the first hotel from The Wolseley Hotels. Set inside the McKim, Mead &amp; White-designed former Lambs Club on West 44th Street, The Wolseley Hotel New York will bring 77 guest rooms and suites to Midtown, alongside a reimagined wellness center and the brand’s first restaurant in the United States. The Wolseley Members Club will add another dimension to the address, with a private, all-day setting for dining, culture and conversation.</p><figure><img alt="" src="https://substack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com/public/images/0fa81f76-8bad-4f8c-b4ac-39ae46a44732_1200x500.jpeg"/><figcaption>Photo Credit: Courtesy of Minor Hotels.</figcaption></figure><p><strong>The Curated Take</strong></p><p>New York has no shortage of luxury hotels, but a property with a historic gathering-place identity and an established restaurant heritage brings a different proposition. The combination of hotel, dining and private club could make this more than somewhere to stay between city engagements.</p>
