<figure><img alt="" src="https://substack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com/public/images/58d479fd-0d33-4919-a26a-bdaba9f56733_1512x530.png"/><figcaption>Photo Credit: Courtesy of Velas Resorts.</figcaption></figure><p>The 31st edition of Festival Gourmet Internacional returns to Puerto Vallarta and Riviera Nayarit with more than 50 chefs and 25 participating restaurants, including special dining experiences at three Velas Resorts properties from November 13–22, 2026. At Grand Velas Riviera Nayarit, Colombian chef Andrés Fernándes León will present a six-course tasting menu inspired by French gastronomy and reinterpreted through Colombian ingredients and his own culinary perspective. Velas Vallarta welcomes Texas chef Victor Howard for a menu of smoked specialties, along with a hands-on cooking class and a beachside barbecue and wine celebration. At adults-only Casa Velas, Nahúm Velasco, Executive Chef of the MICHELIN-starred Cocina de Autor at Grand Velas Riviera Maya, will serve a six-course menu drawing on signature dishes and creations developed for the occasion. Together, the collaborations bring international culinary talent and regional interpretation to the resort experience.</p><p><strong>The Curated Take</strong></p><p>For travelers who plan their journeys around food, a festival like this offers more than a memorable dinner. It creates opportunities to encounter different culinary traditions through the perspectives of visiting chefs, while experiencing the distinct character of each resort. With events ranging from refined tasting menus to cooking classes and a sunset beach barbecue, Velas Resorts gives guests several ways to engage with Puerto Vallarta and Riviera Nayarit’s growing reputation as culinary destinations.</p>
