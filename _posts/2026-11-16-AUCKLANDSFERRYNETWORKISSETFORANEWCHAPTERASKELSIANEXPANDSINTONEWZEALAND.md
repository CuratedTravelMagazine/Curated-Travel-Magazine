<figure><img alt="" src="https://substack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com/public/images/0270b178-4440-48ce-9a36-04f476a09a50_600x900.jpeg"/></figure><p>Australia-based Kelsian Group has formally entered the New Zealand market with the completion of its acquisition of Auckland ferry operator Belaire Ferries, establishing a new foothold in the country’s passenger transport network. The acquisition follows a successful joint bid for Auckland Transport’s expanded Western Package ferry services, scheduled to begin July 1, 2027. Under an initial seven-year contract, Belaire Ferries will operate six vessels serving Bayswater, Birkenhead, Te Onewa/Northcote Point, West Harbour and Rakino Island, with an option to extend for an additional two years. Kelsian also plans to invest approximately A$31 million in five new vessels to support the network over the life of the contract. Local leadership has been appointed as operational preparations get underway, with Philip Molony taking the role of General Manager. The expansion brings together Kelsian’s established marine operations and Belaire’s local knowledge, with the goal of maintaining reliable connections between Auckland’s waterfront communities and surrounding islands.</p><figure><img alt="" src="https://substack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com/public/images/0fa81f76-8bad-4f8c-b4ac-39ae46a44732_1200x500.jpeg"/><figcaption>Photo Credit: Auckland, New Zealand. Courtesy of Kelsian Group.</figcaption></figure><h3>The Curated Take</h3><p>Ferries are more than a practical means of getting around Auckland. They are part of the experience of a harbor city, connecting waterfront neighborhoods and island communities while offering a different perspective on the surrounding landscape. The investment in new vessels and expanded services signals a significant development for Auckland’s transport infrastructure, with potential benefits for both residents and visitors. For travelers, dependable ferry connections can make it easier to explore beyond the central waterfront and discover the communities and coastal settings that give the region its distinctive character.</p>
