<figure><img alt="" src="https://substack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com/public/images/d85021c1-e0a5-4a33-aeb8-74cc264f4aaf_600x338.jpeg"/></figure><p>From the Indian Ocean shoreline of Jimbaran Bay to the lush Ayung River valley, Four Seasons Resorts Bali is inviting travelers to experience a quieter, more contemplative side of the island through The Season of Sacred Water, a collection of wellness, cultural and culinary experiences running from October 2026 through March 2027. Rooted in the spiritual significance of water in Balinese life, the program explores the island’s relationship with nature through traditional herbal drink-making, sound rituals and restorative spa treatments. At Jimbaran Bay, Tides of Renewal draws inspiration from the coast, with jamu-making and a treatment accompanied by the sounds of the ocean and Balinese gongs. At Sayan, Flow into Stillness turns toward the river and forest, pairing conversations about wellbeing with meditative rest in a suspended silk hammock and treatments incorporating local herbs and traditional instruments. Together, the two settings offer a more immersive approach to Bali wellness travel, one shaped by the island’s living traditions and natural landscapes.</p><figure><img alt="" src="https://substack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com/public/images/0fa81f76-8bad-4f8c-b4ac-39ae46a44732_1200x500.jpeg"/><figcaption>Photo Credit: Bali, Indonesia. Courtesy of Four Seasons Resorts Bali.</figcaption></figure><h3>The Curated Take</h3><p>Bali’s appeal has never rested solely in its beaches or beautiful resorts. Its spiritual traditions, relationship with the natural world and distinctive approach to daily rituals offer a deeper dimension of discovery. What makes this collection compelling is the way it interprets those traditions through two very different landscapes. Jimbaran Bay offers the restorative presence of the sea, while Sayan brings guests closer to the rhythms of the river valley. For travelers seeking meaningful wellness experiences, this is an invitation to encounter Bali at a gentler pace, with time to listen, learn and reconnect.</p>
