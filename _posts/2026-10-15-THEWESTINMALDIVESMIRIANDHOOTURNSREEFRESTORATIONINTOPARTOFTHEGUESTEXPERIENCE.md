<p>The Westin Maldives Miriandhoo Resort has begun a new chapter in marine conservation with Coral RISE, a coral restoration initiative on its house reef in Baa Atoll. The first phase, completed in September, installed 100 Reef Stars carrying 1,500 coral fragments using the MARS Assisted Reef Restoration System, a science-based approach designed to provide damaged reef areas with a stable foundation for coral growth and marine habitat. The project is being developed with MARS Sustainable Solutions and will continue with monitoring and maintenance as the reef develops.</p>
<p>What distinguishes the initiative is its connection to the guest experience. The restoration site is accessible for snorkeling, allowing visitors to observe the Reef Stars and follow the progress of the coral over time, while marine education introduces guests to the importance of reef ecosystems in the Maldives. The project also complements the resort’s broader sustainability efforts and its location within the UNESCO Biosphere Reserve of Baa Atoll, where the surrounding marine environment is central to the destination itself.</p>
<p><strong>The Curated Take:</strong> Coral RISE moves conservation from something a resort promotes to something guests can actually see taking shape. That creates a different relationship with the destination: rather than simply enjoying the reef, travelers have an opportunity to understand the work involved in helping restore it and to return to see how that effort evolves.</p>
