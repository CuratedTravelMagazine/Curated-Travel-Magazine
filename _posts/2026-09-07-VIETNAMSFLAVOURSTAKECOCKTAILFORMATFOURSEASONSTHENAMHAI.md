<p>At Four Seasons Resort The Nam Hai in Hoi An, Vietnam’s culinary traditions are moving behind the bar for two nights this September. On September 12 and 13, 2026, Sol &amp; Sao will host The Enigma Mansion, recognized by Asia’s 50 Best Bars, for an exclusive cocktail experience exploring the country’s regional ingredients, seasonal harvests and evolving mixology scene.</p>
<p>Under the theme <strong>“Two Cities, One Night,”</strong> the four-cocktail menu reinterprets familiar Vietnamese flavours through contemporary techniques. Strawberry combines whisky with strawberry whey, koji water, cacao wine and salted apricot; wintermelon pairs gin with cucumber, dill, pear, tonic and mezcal; beetroot brings together whisky, jasmine, Parmesan and tamarind; and a rice-inspired serve combines bourbon, red rice, butter, apple, miso vermouth and passionfruit seed bitters. The experience runs from 6:30 to 10:30 p.m., offering resort guests an opportunity to discover a different side of Vietnam’s culinary culture without leaving the property.</p>
<p><strong>The Curated Take:</strong> The most interesting luxury hotel experiences increasingly go beyond simply showcasing local ingredients. They give travelers a new way to understand them. Here, Vietnamese flavours that might be encountered in a market or kitchen are transformed through modern mixology, connecting regional culinary heritage with the country’s contemporary food and beverage scene. It is a small but compelling example of how a resort can make a destination feel more discoverable, even from behind its own bar.</p>
