<p>Lafayette, Louisiana, is having a moment as a fall destination. The heart of Acadiana placed fifth in the 2026 USA TODAY 10Best Readers’ Choice Awards for Best Place to Visit for Fall, following four weeks of public voting after being selected as one of 20 destinations nominated by a panel of travel experts.</p>
<p>The recognition puts a national spotlight on the elements that make autumn distinctive in Lafayette and the surrounding Acadiana region, from a calendar of fall festivals to the area’s deeply rooted culinary traditions, outdoor experiences and distinctive Cajun and Creole culture. This marks the third consecutive year Lafayette has been nominated and named among USA TODAY’s 10Best fall destinations, suggesting that its appeal extends well beyond the traditional peak travel seasons.</p>
<p><strong>The Curated Take:</strong> Lafayette’s fall appeal is less about seasonal scenery than about what happens on the ground. Festivals, food and cultural traditions give the season a sense of occasion, while the region’s outdoor experiences provide another way to explore Acadiana. For travelers looking for a destination where autumn is expressed through community and culture rather than simply changing leaves, Lafayette offers a distinctly Louisiana interpretation of the season.</p>
