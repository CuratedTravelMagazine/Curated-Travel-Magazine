<figure><img alt="" src="https://substack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com/public/images/7ccb9983-3217-446d-928f-e1e7377d384b_600x341.png"/></figure><p>On July 22, 2028, a total solar eclipse will offer travelers a rare reason to explore New Zealand, and Smithsonian Journeys is building an expert-led journey around the celestial event. Scheduled for July 13–24, 2028, the 12-day experience will cross both the North and South Islands, connecting Auckland, Rotorua, Queenstown and the Southern Alps with an exclusive eclipse viewing celebration at a Gibbston Valley vineyard in Central Otago wine country. Astronomy experts will accompany the journey, offering lectures and discussions that bring the science of the eclipse into conversation with New Zealand’s natural and cultural heritage. The announcement also expands Smithsonian Journeys’ collection of eclipse-focused travel, with additional 2028 departures in Australia and journeys planned for the 2027 total solar eclipse in Spain, Morocco, Egypt and along the Iberian Peninsula. For travelers drawn to rare natural phenomena, the experience offers an opportunity to pair a remarkable moment in the night sky with a deeper exploration of a destination.</p><figure><img alt="" src="https://substack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com/public/images/0fa81f76-8bad-4f8c-b4ac-39ae46a44732_1200x500.jpeg"/><figcaption>Photo Credit: Courtesy of Smithsonian Journeys.</figcaption></figure><h3>The Curated Take</h3><p>Celestial events have a way of giving a journey a sense of occasion, but the most rewarding eclipse trips are about more than securing a place beneath the path of totality. The appeal of this New Zealand itinerary lies in the combination of expert interpretation, regional discovery and the setting itself. Central Otago’s vineyard landscapes provide a distinctive backdrop for the viewing, while the wider journey offers an introduction to two islands with very different natural and cultural character. With the 2027 and 2028 eclipses inspiring a growing range of expert-led journeys, astronomy is becoming an intriguing lens through which to experience the world.</p>
