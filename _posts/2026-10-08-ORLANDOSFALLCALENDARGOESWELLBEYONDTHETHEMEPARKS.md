<p>Orlando’s fall calendar is making the destination relevant to travelers for reasons that extend well beyond its famous theme parks. From the Southern Hill Farms Fall Festival and Winter Park Autumn Art Festival to the Central Florida Veg Fest, EPCOT International Food &amp; Wine Festival and Swan and Dolphin Food &amp; Wine Classic, autumn brings a diverse mix of food, art, seasonal traditions and cultural celebrations. The city’s 94-day Halloween season adds another layer, while events such as FusionFest and Fiesta Calle Orange reflect the many cultures that shape Central Florida.</p>
<p>Music and sports give fall travelers another reason to build a trip around a particular date. Electric Daisy Carnival returns to Tinker Field, the Vans Warped Tour brings punk, alternative and emo music to Camping World Stadium, and concerts by artists including Doja Cat, Olivia Rodrigo and SOMBR add major touring acts to the calendar. Meanwhile, the Orlando Magic, Orlando Solar Bears, Orlando City SC and Orlando Pride offer professional basketball, hockey and soccer. Even the Kennedy Space Center Visitor Complex joins the culinary calendar with Taste of Space: Fall Bites!, adding a distinctly Central Florida twist to the season.</p>
<p><strong>The Curated Take:</strong> Orlando’s fall appeal is increasingly about choosing your own reason to go. A traveler might come for a food festival, an art weekend, a major concert, a soccer match or Halloween, with the city’s broader dining, entertainment and cultural landscape filling in the itinerary. That makes fall less a single Orlando experience and more a collection of distinct ways to experience the destination.</p>
