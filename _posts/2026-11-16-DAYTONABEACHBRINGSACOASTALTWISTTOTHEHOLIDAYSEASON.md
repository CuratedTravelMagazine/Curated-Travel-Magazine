<figure><img alt="" src="https://substack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com/public/images/014b08d5-6e2f-4c6b-ae5f-636fe569087f_600x600.jpeg"/></figure><p>Holiday traditions take on a distinctly Florida character in Daytona Beach, where the Atlantic coastline provides a sunny backdrop for seasonal celebrations, waterfront gatherings and a calendar of festive events. From November through early January, the destination pairs its 23 miles of beaches with illuminated attractions, live performances and long-standing community traditions. At Daytona International Speedway, Magic of Lights transforms the racing venue with more than two million lights and an illuminated tunnel, while the expanded five-day Daytona Turkey Run draws classic-car enthusiasts for one of the country’s largest automotive gatherings. Elsewhere, visitors can skate beside the ocean at the Renaissance Daytona Beach Oceanfront Hotel, explore the sparkling Riverfront Esplanade in downtown Daytona Beach, or take in holiday performances ranging from The Nutcracker to Mannheim Steamroller Christmas. Nearby DeLand adds another seasonal highlight with the elaborate holiday tour of the historic Stetson Mansion. Together, these experiences make the Daytona Beach area an appealing setting for a Thanksgiving getaway, Christmas vacation or winter escape along Florida’s Atlantic coast.</p><figure><img alt="" src="https://substack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com/public/images/0fa81f76-8bad-4f8c-b4ac-39ae46a44732_1200x500.jpeg"/><figcaption>Photo Credit: Daytona Beach, Florida. Courtesy of the Daytona Beach Area Convention &amp; Visitors Bureau.</figcaption></figure><h3>The Curated Take</h3><p>Daytona Beach offers an alternative to the traditional winter holiday destination, trading snow-covered streets for ocean views while preserving the rituals that make the season feel special. The contrast is part of its appeal: an outdoor ice rink beside the Atlantic, holiday lights along the Halifax River, classic cars gathered at a legendary motorsports venue and performances in historic cultural spaces. For travelers who want festive experiences without giving up time outdoors, the destination brings together the energy of a holiday event calendar and the easygoing pleasures of a Florida coastal stay.</p>
