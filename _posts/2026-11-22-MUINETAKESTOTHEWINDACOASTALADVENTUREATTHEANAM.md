<figure><img alt="" src="https://substack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com/public/images/ec2dc462-03d1-49d6-ae22-f16bc598c04e_961x919.jpeg"/></figure><p>From November through March, the Northeast Monsoon transforms Mui Ne, Vietnam, into a spectacular setting for kitesurfing, with colorful sails filling the skies above its coastline. The Anam Mui Ne is introducing a seasonal experience that brings guests closer to this distinctive coastal culture through its Windswept Adrenaline Tour. The half-day excursion begins with a private Jeep safari along the shore, continuing to Hon Rom Beach for views of the kitesurfers before a private introductory workshop at Ca Ty Beach, where certified instructors demonstrate the fundamentals of kite control on land. An expert freestyle performance follows, offering a closer look at the sport’s skill and spectacle. The afternoon concludes with a private sunset picnic by the sea. Designed for beginners as well as curious observers, the experience offers a fresh way to discover the winds, scenery, and outdoor culture that have made Mui Ne one of Asia’s leading kitesurfing destinations.</p><figure><img alt="" src="https://substack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com/public/images/0fa81f76-8bad-4f8c-b4ac-39ae46a44732_1200x500.jpeg"/><figcaption>Photo Credit: Mui Ne, Vietnam. Courtesy of The Anam Mui Ne.</figcaption></figure><h3>The Curated Take</h3><p>Some destinations are defined by a particular relationship with the elements. In Mui Ne, the seasonal winds are more than a feature of the weather; they have shaped the coastline’s identity and drawn an international community of kitesurfers. The Anam’s experience offers an accessible introduction to that local character, balancing the energy of the sport with the quieter pleasure of watching the sails move across the water as daylight fades. It is a reminder that a memorable resort experience can begin by looking beyond the property and discovering what makes its surroundings distinctive.</p>
