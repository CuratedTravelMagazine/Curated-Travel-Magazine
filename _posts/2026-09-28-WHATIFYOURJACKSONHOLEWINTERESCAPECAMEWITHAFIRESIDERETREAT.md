<p>In Jackson Hole, Wyoming, winter is as much about what happens after the adventure as it is about the snow itself. At the gateway to Grand Teton National Park, the 40-room Rusty Parrot Lodge &amp; Spa offers a boutique base for experiencing the season, with world-class skiing, wildlife encounters, sleigh rides and the dramatic winter landscapes of the Tetons all close by.</p>
<p>Back at the lodge, the pace shifts from outdoor adventure to restorative comfort. Guests can unwind with treatments at Body Sage Spa, soak in the jacuzzi or settle in for seasonally inspired cuisine and fireside cocktails at the award-winning Wild Sage Restaurant &amp; Bar. The intimate scale of the property keeps the experience distinctly personal, creating a retreat that feels connected to Jackson Hole rather than simply positioned within it.</p>
<p><strong>The Curated Take:</strong> Jackson Hole has no shortage of luxury accommodations, but the appeal here is the balance between access and intimacy. Rusty Parrot makes the surrounding winter landscape the reason to venture out, then gives travelers a quieter, more indulgent place to return to. For travelers who want the full mountain experience without sacrificing a sense of retreat, that rhythm is part of the luxury.</p>
