<p>The Swan Valley, one of Western Australia’s oldest wine regions, will welcome the return of <strong>Entwined in the Valley</strong> from October 5–18, 2026. The two-week celebration invites visitors to explore the region through a program of food, wine, heritage and hands-on experiences, with events ranging from long-table lunches among the vines to cellar-door visits, workshops and immersive masterclasses.</p>
<p>The festival offers a broad introduction to the Swan Valley’s culinary and agricultural character, combining behind-the-scenes tours and wine experiences with live entertainment and family-friendly activities. For travelers based in Perth, it provides an opportunity to venture into the nearby wine region, while those planning a longer Western Australian itinerary can use the event as a way to explore the area’s producers and historic landscapes.</p>
<p><strong>The Curated Take:</strong><br/>Entwined is a reminder that wine regions are often best understood through more than tastings alone. By bringing together food, viticulture, heritage and participation, the festival gives visitors a fuller sense of the Swan Valley as a living agricultural and cultural landscape.</p>
