<p>Mulia Bali is turning the holiday season into a full resort experience, with Christmas celebrations, family programming, festive dining and a lavish New Year’s Eve finale planned for 2026. Set along the shores of Nusa Dua, the resort’s festivities culminate December 31 with <strong>“A Night in Elysium,”</strong> a gala in the Grand Ballroom featuring international performers, elaborate dining and a midnight countdown designed to carry the celebration into 2027.</p>
<p>The evening’s entertainment brings together The Starlets, whose performers have appeared at major international events and in West End productions including <em>Hamilton</em> and <em>TINA – The Tina Turner Musical</em>, with Australian live band Fiction. Across the wider festive calendar, guests can choose from Christmas Eve and Christmas Day feasts, countdown dinners at the resort’s restaurants and a kids program of creative workshops and holiday activities. Family packages, breakfast-inclusive direct-booking benefits and a dining gift-card promotion add further incentives for a holiday stay.</p>
<p><strong>The Curated Take:</strong><br/>For travelers looking to make the holiday season an actual destination experience rather than simply a change of scenery, Bali offers an appealing combination of tropical surroundings and traditional year-end celebration. Mulia’s program is particularly geared toward multigenerational travelers, pairing a substantial entertainment lineup and gala atmosphere with dedicated children’s programming and multiple dining options.</p>
