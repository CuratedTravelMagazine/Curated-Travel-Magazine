<p>The Perth Mint has claimed a Guinness World Records title with a gold bar weighing an extraordinary <strong>521.2 kilograms</strong> and refined to 99.999 percent purity. The record-setting bar represents a technical achievement for the historic mint, which has been operating for 127 years, and adds another remarkable object to its public collection alongside its record-breaking One Tonne Gold Coin.</p>
<p>The bar will become part of The Perth Mint’s Exhibition, giving visitors an opportunity to see an object that combines Australia’s long association with gold with the technical expertise of one of the country’s best-known precious-metals institutions. It is an unexpected addition to Perth’s cultural attractions and an especially striking stop for travelers interested in the history, craftsmanship and industry behind Australia’s gold story.</p>
<p><strong>The Curated Take:</strong><br/>There is a satisfying travel story hiding behind what initially sounds like a novelty record. The world’s largest gold bar connects Perth’s present-day technical capabilities with the city’s deep history as a center of Australia’s gold industry, making it more than simply an enormous piece of precious metal.</p>
