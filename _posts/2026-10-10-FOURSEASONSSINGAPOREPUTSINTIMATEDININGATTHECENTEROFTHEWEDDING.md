<p>For couples choosing a wedding built around a smaller circle of family and friends, Four Seasons Hotel Singapore is placing intimate dining and distinctive settings at the heart of the celebration. On the hotel’s 20th floor, Windows East and Windows West offer private venues with floor-to-ceiling windows, outdoor patios and panoramic views across Singapore, creating an elevated setting for ceremonies, cocktails and dinners of up to 100 guests.</p>
<p>The experience extends beyond the venue itself, with individually plated six- and seven-course menus from MICHELIN-Selected Jiang-Nan Chun and opportunities to gather at Nobu Bar for welcome cocktails, pre-wedding celebrations or late-night toasts. Couples can also make one of the hotel’s restaurants the setting itself, with exclusive buyouts available at Nobu, Jiang-Nan Chun and One-Ninety. The result is a wedding experience shaped as much by food and atmosphere as by the ceremony, with each restaurant offering a distinctly different culinary and social character.</p>
<p><strong>The Curated Take:</strong> Intimate weddings are increasingly less about scaling down a traditional celebration and more about designing something deliberately personal. Four Seasons Singapore’s approach plays into that shift by treating dining as part of the occasion’s identity rather than simply another element of the reception. For couples who would rather spend an evening around one beautifully considered table than host a large ballroom affair, the combination of Singapore views, restaurant-level cuisine and private spaces offers a more intimate way to celebrate without sacrificing a sense of occasion.</p>
