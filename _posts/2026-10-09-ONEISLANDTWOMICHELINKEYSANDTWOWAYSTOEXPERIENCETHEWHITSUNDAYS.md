<p>Hamilton Island has become home to two MICHELIN Key hotels, giving the small Whitsunday island an unusual concentration of internationally recognized stays. Adults-only qualia has retained its MICHELIN Key for a second consecutive year, while The Sundays, a 59-room boutique hotel that opened in 2025, has received its first. The distinction recognizes hotels for qualities including design, service, character, value and connection to place, and in Hamilton Island’s case, the two properties offer distinctly different interpretations of island hospitality.</p>
<p>At qualia, privacy, personalized service and access to the natural environment remain central, with experiences extending from Pebble Beach dining and wellness to private excursions around Whitehaven Beach, Heart Island and the Great Barrier Reef. The Sundays takes a more social, family-focused approach, with its Catseye Beach setting, waterside pool and Catseye Pool Club, the restaurant from Australian chefs Josh and Julie Niland. Together, the properties reflect the broader evolution of Hamilton Island, where luxury is increasingly expressed through different kinds of experiences rather than a single resort model.</p>
<p><strong>The Curated Take:</strong> The more interesting story here is not simply that Hamilton Island has two MICHELIN Keys. It is that one destination can accommodate two very different versions of the Australian island escape. qualia is built around seclusion and refinement, while The Sundays makes togetherness and family time part of the experience. Both ultimately draw their character from the same setting: the Whitsundays and the extraordinary waters of the Great Barrier Reef.</p>
