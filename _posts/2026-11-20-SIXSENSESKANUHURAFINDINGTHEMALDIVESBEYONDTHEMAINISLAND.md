Set amid lush tropical surroundings and white-sand beaches, the resort features understated beachfront and overwater villas built with natural materials, while two neighboring deserted islands provide opportunities to venture beyond the main property. Guests can explore uninhabited shores, snorkel vibrant coral reefs and enjoy personalized wellness rituals, with each experience encouraging a slower connection to the surrounding environment. For travelers seeking a quieter perspective on the Maldives, Kanuhura offers more than the familiar appeal of a private island stay. Courtesy of Six Senses Kanuhura.</figcaption></figure><h3>The Curated Take</h3><p>The appeal of a remote island is often measured by what lies just beyond its shores. At Six Senses Kanuhura, access to two neighboring deserted islands adds an element of exploration to the traditional Maldivian retreat. The experience is less about filling each day with activity and more about having the freedom to follow the water, discover a quieter stretch of sand or simply enjoy the luxury of time.</p>
