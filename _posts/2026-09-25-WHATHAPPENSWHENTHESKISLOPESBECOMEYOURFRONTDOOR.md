<p>In Oberlech, a car-free village high in Austria’s Arlberg, winter begins where the road ends. Once snow arrives, the village is accessible only by cable car, placing Burg Hotel Lech at 1,650 meters in the heart of one of the Alps’ most celebrated ski regions. Surrounded by 300 kilometers of interconnected slopes, the hotel offers true ski-in, ski-out access, allowing guests to move from their room to the mountain without the logistics that can turn a ski vacation into a production.</p>
<p>The experience continues well after the lifts close. At Burg Eisbar No. 1, a slope-side ice bar with a heated terrace, retractable roof and live music, après-ski can stretch into the evening. For a more traditional Alpine atmosphere, Burg Schenke serves fondue in a rustic tavern setting, while the adults-only Burg Oasis offers a quieter retreat with a stone pine sauna, snow cabin and fireplace whirlpool. Together, the spaces give a stay at Burg Hotel Lech several distinct ways to experience winter, from first tracks on the mountain to a slower evening indoors.</p>
<p><strong>The Curated Take:</strong> The appeal of Burg Hotel Lech is the way its setting shapes the entire stay. The car-free village and ski-in, ski-out access eliminate much of the friction associated with a mountain getaway, while the hotel’s après-ski, dining and wellness spaces create reasons to linger once skiing is over. It is a distinctly Alpine version of luxury where convenience comes from being completely immersed in the landscape.</p>
