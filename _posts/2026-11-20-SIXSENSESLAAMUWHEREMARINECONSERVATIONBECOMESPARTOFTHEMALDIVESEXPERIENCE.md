Surrounded by white-sand beaches, turquoise lagoons and thriving coral reefs, the resort’s beachfront and overwater villas are designed to blend with their natural surroundings. Its Maldives Underwater Initiative gives guests an opportunity to explore the marine environment alongside resident biologists, learning about ongoing efforts to protect sea turtles, manta rays and fragile reef ecosystems. These encounters add a meaningful dimension to a stay in the Indian Ocean, inviting travelers to look beyond the familiar pleasures of a tropical escape and develop a deeper understanding of the waters and wildlife that define this remote corner of the Maldives.</p><figure><img alt="" src="https://substack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com/public/images/0fa81f76-8bad-4f8c-b4ac-39ae46a44732_1200x500.jpeg"/><figcaption>Photo Credit: Laamu Atoll, Maldives. Courtesy of Six Senses Laamu.</figcaption></figure><h3>The Curated Take</h3><p>The Maldives is often experienced from the surface, through overwater villas, turquoise lagoons and days spent on the beach. At Six Senses Laamu, the opportunity to learn from marine biologists offers another way to understand the destination. Conservation becomes part of the travel experience itself, giving a stay here a sense of purpose that extends beyond the beauty of the setting.</p>
