<p>At Four Seasons Resort The Nam Hai in Hoi An, the festive season takes on a distinctly Vietnamese character. From December 22, 2026 through January 1, 2027, the all-villa resort will combine familiar holiday traditions with experiences rooted in Central Vietnam, from the Thap Nen candle-lighting ceremony and an Enchanted Chocolate Garden exploring Vietnam’s cacao traditions to Christmas dining and a Midnight Forest New Year’s Eve celebration beneath the tropical canopy. The setting, with private pools, gardens and views of the sea, allows families and friends to move between celebration and quiet time at their own pace.</p>
<p>The season also extends beyond the resort itself, with Hoi An Ancient Town, My Son Sanctuary and the Imperial City of Hue offering access to the region’s layered cultural heritage. Guests can explore Hoi An by Vespa, visit a fishing village by bamboo basket boat or incorporate seasonal wellness into their stay at the lotus-filled Heart of the Earth Spa, where new Khoi Sac treatments draw on Vietnamese traditions of renewal. Private villas, including the three-bedroom ocean-view option, provide a residential setting for multigenerational gatherings while preserving space for individual retreat.</p>
<p><strong>The Curated Take:</strong> The appeal here is less about recreating a conventional Christmas in a tropical setting and more about allowing the holiday season to become part of the destination. Vietnamese rituals, Central Vietnam’s cultural heritage, local ingredients and the resort’s distinctive coastal setting give familiar celebrations a sense of place.</p>
