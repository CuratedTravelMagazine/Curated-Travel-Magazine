<p>On Antigua’s southern coast, Christmas arrives with white-sand beaches, turquoise waters, and the warmth of Caribbean island life. At Carlisle Bay, the festive season runs from December 23 through January 3, bringing together holiday dining, wellness, sporting activities, and celebrations for all ages. Families can enjoy the Cool Kids Club’s seasonal program, with gingerbread decorating, beach bonfires and s’mores, a festive Christmas parade featuring Santa, and outdoor movies beneath the stars. Beyond the resort, island adventures range from swimming with rays at Stingray City to ziplining through the rainforest. As the year draws to a close, the celebrations take a glamorous turn with a Saturday Night Fever-themed New Year’s Eve, beginning with cocktails and live entertainment before a disco-inspired gala dinner, dancing, Champagne, and fireworks over the bay. A New Year’s Day steel-band barbecue continues the festivities, offering a sun-filled alternative to the traditional winter holiday.</p>
<h3>The Curated Take</h3>
<p>A festive escape in the Caribbean offers more than a change of climate. It creates space to experience familiar holiday rituals in a setting where the beach becomes the gathering place and celebrations stretch into warm evenings beside the sea. At Carlisle Bay, family adventures and playful seasonal traditions meet the glamour of a disco-inspired New Year’s Eve, making Antigua an appealing choice for travelers who want their holidays to feel both restorative and full of life. Here, the season is less about escaping Christmas than discovering a different way to celebrate it.</p>
