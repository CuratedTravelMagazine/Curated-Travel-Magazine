<figure><img alt="" src="https://substack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com/public/images/d62487f0-5f09-4370-ab03-3e82384e9693_600x450.jpeg"/></figure><p>For travelers who want the freedom of a private residence without giving up the attentive service of a luxury hotel, Casa Chameleon at Las Catalinas is introducing a new way to experience Costa Rica’s Pacific coast. Beginning January 2027, four exclusive five-bedroom branded residences will join the property’s offering, perched along a scenic ridgeline above the car-free coastal town of Las Catalinas in Guanacaste. Each villa accommodates up to 10 guests and features a private oceanview plunge pool, rooftop garden, multiple terraces and expansive indoor-outdoor living spaces. Managed by the team behind the Relais &amp; Châteaux member and Two Michelin Key boutique hotel, the residences offer dedicated concierge service, access to the hotel’s dining experiences and assistance arranging private chefs, in-residence spa treatments and personalized activities. Guests can also enjoy the surrounding destination, from protected tropical forest and coastal trails to the Pacific’s sheltered bays, wellness offerings and the walkable town itself.</p><figure><img alt="" src="https://substack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com/public/images/0fa81f76-8bad-4f8c-b4ac-39ae46a44732_1200x500.jpeg"/><figcaption>Photo Credit: Las Catalinas, Guanacaste, Costa Rica. Courtesy of Casa Chameleon at Las Catalinas.</figcaption></figure><h3>The Curated Take</h3><p>The appeal of a private villa is often the freedom to gather, settle into a destination and create a rhythm of one’s own. Casa Chameleon adds another layer by bringing the service culture and hospitality of an established luxury hotel into a residential setting. With just four residences, the experience is particularly suited to multigenerational families, milestone celebrations and groups of friends who value both shared spaces and personal privacy. Las Catalinas also offers a distinctive setting, where a car-free coastal town, protected natural surroundings and access to outdoor experiences create a sense of place beyond the villa itself.</p>
