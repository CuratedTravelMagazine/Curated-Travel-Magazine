Japanese art collective **teamLab** is bringing its immersive art experience to Anaheim, California, with plans for its first permanent U.S. museum at OCVIBE in 2030. The approximately 110,000-square-foot museum will feature interactive installations that respond to visitors, making people an active part of the artwork rather than simply observers. Designed by Smith-Clementi, the museum will be part of OCVIBE, a developing arts, entertainment, dining, and hospitality district near the Honda Center.

**The Curated Take**

For travelers who have experienced teamLab’s celebrated installations in Japan, this brings a new opportunity closer to home. For California, it adds a distinctive cultural attraction to a destination increasingly bringing art, entertainment, and hospitality together. The larger appeal is the chance to experience art as something immersive and participatory, not simply something viewed from a distance.
