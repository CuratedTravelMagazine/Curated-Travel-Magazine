<figure><img alt="" src="https://substack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com/public/images/6c758f13-a9a5-4b80-bfab-cfc169cecd6b_600x500.jpeg"/></figure><p>At Holiday Inn Resort Kandooma Maldives, an all-inclusive island escape is expanding well beyond meals and beverages to encompass the experiences that make a Maldives holiday memorable. The 146-villa resort has introduced an experience-led offering that brings together ocean adventures, diving, traditional fishing, wellness, and island dining. Qualifying stays include a sunset cruise, daily group reef snorkeling, sunrise line fishing, and one scuba dive per day for certified divers, along with a choice of massage at Kandooma Spa by COMO Shambhala. With 28 dive sites accessible from its PADI 5-Star Dive Centre, including the renowned Kandooma Thila, the resort offers opportunities to explore the marine life of the South Malé Atoll. Guests can also enjoy dining across multiple island venues, from casual poolside meals to beachside dining and a 24-hour café, alongside a changing program of activities for adults and children. The approach reflects a broader idea of all-inclusive travel: making it easier for guests to discover more of the destination without having to plan every experience separately.</p><figure><img alt="" src="https://substack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com/public/images/0fa81f76-8bad-4f8c-b4ac-39ae46a44732_1200x500.jpeg"/><figcaption>Photo Credit: Kandooma Maldives, South Malé Atoll, Maldives. Courtesy of Kandooma Maldives.</figcaption></figure><h3>The Curated Take</h3><p>The Maldives is often associated with private villas and pristine beaches, but the most memorable island stays offer reasons to venture beyond the shoreline. Kandooma’s expanded all-inclusive concept puts the surrounding ocean and local traditions at the center of the experience, with sunrise fishing, reef exploration, and diving woven into the rhythm of a stay. The inclusion of wellness and family activities adds another dimension, making the resort an appealing option for travelers who want both relaxation and a sense of discovery. It is an approach that makes the destination itself part of the holiday, rather than simply the backdrop.</p>
