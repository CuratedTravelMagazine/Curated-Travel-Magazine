<figure><img alt="" src="https://substack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com/public/images/bba1f92a-dffc-4203-b299-0cf11fda3f6a_600x800.jpeg"/></figure><p>Thanksgiving takes on a distinctly Baja California character at Las Ventanas al Paraíso, A Rosewood Resort, where a weeklong Season of Gratitude from November 21–28, 2026, brings families together through regional traditions, culinary experiences, and time along the coast. At the center of the celebrations is a Thanksgiving dinner at Alebrije, featuring a family-style menu that brings together holiday favorites, French and Italian influences, and ingredients from local producers. Beyond the table, the program invites guests of different generations to take part in Mexican craft activities, traditional Lotería, cooking challenges, and garden explorations. Teenagers have their own creative and social experiences, while families can enjoy beach activities, evening s’mores, stargazing, and a movie night beneath the coastal sky. Set where the desert landscape meets the Sea of Cortez, the resort offers a festive holiday escape shaped by food, culture, and shared experiences.</p><figure><img alt="" src="https://substack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com/public/images/0fa81f76-8bad-4f8c-b4ac-39ae46a44732_1200x500.jpeg"/><figcaption>Photo Credit: Las Ventanas al Paraíso, A Rosewood Resort, Los Cabos, Baja California Sur, Mexico. Courtesy of Rosewood Hotels &amp; Resorts.</figcaption></figure><h3>The Curated Take</h3><p>Thanksgiving travel can be about more than recreating the familiar holiday somewhere warmer. Las Ventanas al Paraíso offers a different way to celebrate, bringing Mexican traditions and local ingredients into a gathering that still honors the pleasures of the Thanksgiving table. The multigenerational programming is particularly appealing, giving children, teenagers, and adults their own ways to participate while creating opportunities to come together. For families considering a luxury Thanksgiving getaway in Los Cabos, the experience makes the holiday feel both recognizable and refreshingly connected to its setting.</p>
