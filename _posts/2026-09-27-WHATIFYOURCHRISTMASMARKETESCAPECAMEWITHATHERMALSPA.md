<p>For travelers seeking a quieter alternative to London during the holidays, Bath, England offers a festive escape built around historic streets, seasonal markets and the restorative tradition of its natural thermal waters. From November 26 through December 13, 2026, the city’s annual Christmas Market will fill the center with more than 170 illuminated chalets featuring festive food, handmade goods and personalized gifts, with sustainability woven into the market’s offerings. The cobbled streets and Georgian architecture provide an atmospheric setting for winter walks and mulled wine.</p>
<p>Just a five-minute stroll from the market, The Gainsborough Bath Spa offers a more restorative side of the season. The five-star hotel is the only property in Bath with access to the city’s natural thermal waters, incorporating them into its spa experience through pools and treatments. After exploring the Christmas market, travelers can return to the hotel to soak in the same thermal tradition that has made Bath a destination for wellness and bathing for centuries, creating a holiday itinerary that balances festive discovery with time to slow down.</p>
<p><strong>The Curated Take:</strong> Bath works particularly well for travelers who want Christmas atmosphere without making the holiday entirely about crowds and spectacle. The combination of a historic English city, a thoughtfully curated market and access to natural thermal waters gives the experience a distinct rhythm. Rather than simply adding a spa day to a Christmas trip, Bath makes bathing part of the destination’s identity, giving a winter city break an unusually restorative dimension.</p>
