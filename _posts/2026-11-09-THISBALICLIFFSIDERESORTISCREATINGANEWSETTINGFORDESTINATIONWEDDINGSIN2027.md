<figure><img alt="" src="https://substack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com/public/images/3aabf42a-1ca7-4fd2-b238-6ea1cc252568_600x399.jpeg"/></figure><p>Overlooking the Indian Ocean from Bali’s dramatic Uluwatu coastline, Karma Kandara is introducing a 2027 collection of bespoke wedding and celebration experiences designed around the island’s natural beauty. The portfolio encompasses everything from intimate proposals and pre-wedding photography to beachfront ceremonies and clifftop receptions, with four distinctive settings: the ocean-facing Sarang Lounge, the resort’s Main Infinity Pool, di Mare Restaurant, and the white sands of Karma Beach Bali. Couples can envision a barefoot celebration beside the sea, an elegant reception framed by panoramic ocean views, or a striking ceremony staged above the water. Beyond weddings, the collection also accommodates milestone occasions and private gatherings, with tailored event planning and experiences that make the most of Uluwatu’s coastal landscape.</p><figure><img alt="" src="https://substack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com/public/images/0fa81f76-8bad-4f8c-b4ac-39ae46a44732_1200x500.jpeg"/><figcaption>Photo Credit: Karma Kandara, Uluwatu, Bali, Indonesia.</figcaption></figure><h3>The Curated Take</h3><p>A destination wedding becomes more meaningful when its setting feels inseparable from the place itself. At Karma Kandara, the contrast between white-sand shoreline, dramatic cliffs, and expansive ocean views gives couples several ways to shape a celebration around Bali rather than simply bring a wedding format somewhere tropical. The range of venues also makes the resort worth considering for couples seeking an intimate gathering or a larger celebration, with the Indian Ocean providing a memorable backdrop at every scale.</p>
