The award highlights opportunities well beyond the classic routes to Machu Picchu, including surfing along the northern coast, trekking through the snow-covered peaks of the Cordillera Blanca in Áncash, exploring the forests and waterfalls of Amazonas, and crossing the desert dunes surrounding Huacachina in Ica. Further afield, travelers can follow high-altitude routes around Ausangate, explore the Salkantay region or venture into the rainforest of Tambopata. Peru is also drawing attention through international sporting events such as the Ultra-Trail Cordillera Blanca and Adidas Andes Race, while PROMPERÚ’s Peru Outdoor Expo is connecting international travel professionals with emerging adventure experiences and destinations throughout the country.</p><figure><img alt="" src="https://substack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com/public/images/0fa81f76-8bad-4f8c-b4ac-39ae46a44732_1200x500.jpeg"/><figcaption>Photo Credit: Peru. Courtesy of PROMPERÚ.</figcaption></figure><h3>The Curated Take</h3><p>Peru’s appeal for adventure travelers extends beyond its iconic archaeological heritage. Its extraordinary geographic range creates opportunities to move between mountain trekking, desert exploration, coastal pursuits and rainforest encounters, often with a strong connection to local communities and landscapes. For travelers who have already experienced the familiar highlights, regions such as Áncash and Amazonas offer a compelling reason to return with a different perspective. The recognition also reinforces the value of looking beyond established itineraries to discover the many distinct journeys that make up Peru.</p>
