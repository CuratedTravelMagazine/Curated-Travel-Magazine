<p>Autumn brings a different rhythm to Portugal’s Algarve, where warm days, golden coastlines and fewer crowds make the season particularly appealing for a coastal escape. At W Algarve in Albufeira, the change of season is an invitation to spend more time outdoors, from exploring the region’s dramatic cliffs to getting out on the water.</p>
<p>The hotel’s Whatever/Whenever service can arrange experiences that take advantage of the landscape, including coasteering and cliff jumping, private kayak excursions into the Benagil caves, and surfing lessons along the Algarve coast. Closer to the hotel, Evaristo and Castelo beaches offer a quieter way to experience the shoreline, with scenic cliff walks and broad views over the Atlantic. Back at the resort, newly added padel, pickleball and tennis courts bring another dimension to an active autumn stay.</p>
<p><strong>The Curated Take:</strong> The Algarve is at its most interesting when the intensity of summer begins to ease. Autumn makes room for a more leisurely relationship with the coast, whether that means exploring its caves and cliffs, walking between beaches or lingering outdoors without the pressure of peak-season crowds. W Algarve provides a contemporary base for experiencing that quieter side of southern Portugal.</p>
