<p>Winter in Charleston offers a different kind of seasonal escape: cooler temperatures, fewer crowds and no snow in sight. Across the harbor from historic downtown Charleston, The Beach Club at Charleston Harbor Resort &amp; Marina in Mount Pleasant provides a waterfront base for experiencing the Lowcountry at a quieter time of year. The 92-room boutique hotel overlooks the harbor, Arthur Ravenel Bridge and the USS Yorktown, the historic World War II aircraft carrier that creates an especially distinctive backdrop from the property’s Charleston Harbor Fish House.</p>
<p>The resort adds its own seasonal traditions to the waterfront setting. From Thanksgiving through mid-January, a family-friendly outdoor ice rink brings a touch of winter wonderland to the harbor, while the Oysters on the Point series returns every other week from December through March with locally steamed oysters, live music and craft beer beside the water. Together, the experiences offer a distinctly Charleston version of winter, pairing the pleasures of the season with the mild-weather appeal of the Lowcountry.</p>
<p><strong>The Curated Take:</strong> The appeal here is the contrast. Charleston doesn’t need to manufacture a snowy winter experience, and The Beach Club embraces that by pairing waterfront views, local seafood and outdoor gatherings with the unexpected addition of ice skating. For travelers who want some seasonal atmosphere without giving up milder weather, it offers a playful way to experience Charleston beyond the traditional high season.</p>
