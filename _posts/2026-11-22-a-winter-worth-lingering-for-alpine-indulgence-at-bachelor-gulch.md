At The Ritz-Carlton, Bachelor Gulch, winter in Colorado’s Beaver Creek mountains extends well beyond the ski slopes. The 2026/27 season brings together alpine adventure, culinary discovery, restorative wellness, and festive traditions in a setting designed for both exhilaration and lingering fireside moments. Ski-in, ski-out access to Beaver Creek Mountain provides a natural starting point, while new experiences invite guests to explore another side of mountain life: a hands-on Cocktail Atelier with the resort’s lead mixologists, a guided Dom Pérignon and caviar tasting, and a private raclette dinner at historic Anderson Cabin. The resort’s 21,000-square-foot spa offers another kind of winter retreat, with therapeutic treatments, sound healing, red-light LED therapy, and restorative facilities. Beyond the slopes, guided snowshoe excursions and hands-on Art in Nature workshops offer opportunities to engage with the landscape at a gentler pace. Seasonal celebrations add to the atmosphere, from the annual tree lighting and holiday programming to Hanukkah observances and New Year’s Eve dining.

### The Curated Take

The appeal of a mountain escape is not always measured in vertical feet or hours spent on the slopes. At Bachelor Gulch, the winter experience is shaped by the contrast between outdoor adventure and the pleasures of staying in: a private alpine dinner, a carefully guided tasting, or an evening gathered around a three-story stone fireplace. That balance gives the destination a broader appeal, making it as inviting for travelers drawn to the rituals of winter as for those who come specifically to ski.
