<figure><img alt="" src="https://substack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com/public/images/1b61d241-ff23-4fde-b8cc-22de64c1c57e_600x400.jpeg"/></figure><p>Australia’s most meaningful cultural journeys often begin with the people who know its landscapes best. The latest edition of Connect to Country, the digital publication from Discover Aboriginal Experiences, invites travelers to explore the country through the knowledge, traditions and perspectives of Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander guides. Issue 12 journeys from the ancient rock art landscapes and rainforests of the Worimi people to sunrise experiences at Uluru, a Djiringanj ceremony on Yuin Country in New South Wales, and an early morning cruise through Kakadu’s Yellow Water Billabong. Other stories explore the enduring relationship between coastal communities and oysters, the history of the pearl trade on Western Australia’s Dampier Peninsula, and the ways Traditional Custodians have sustained their cultures through generations of change. The edition also connects luxury travelers with First Nations-led experiences paired with stays from Luxury Lodges of Australia, demonstrating how cultural understanding can become an essential part of a sophisticated Australian travel experience.</p><figure><img alt="" src="https://substack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com/public/images/0fa81f76-8bad-4f8c-b4ac-39ae46a44732_1200x500.jpeg"/><figcaption>Photo Credit: Courtesy of Discover Aboriginal Experiences and Tourism Australia.</figcaption></figure><h3>The Curated Take</h3><p>Cultural travel becomes richer when the experience moves beyond observing a place to understanding the people, histories and traditions that have shaped it. First Nations-led tourism offers an opportunity to encounter Australia through perspectives that cannot be found in a conventional destination guide, from ancient knowledge of the natural environment to stories carried through dance, food, language and ceremony. For luxury travelers, these experiences bring depth and context to journeys through Australia’s extraordinary landscapes, while placing the voices of Traditional Custodians at the center of the story.</p>
