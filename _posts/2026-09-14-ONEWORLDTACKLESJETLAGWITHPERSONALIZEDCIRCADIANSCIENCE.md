<p>Round the World travel may promise the ultimate multi-destination adventure, but crossing multiple time zones can quickly take a toll. Now, oneworld is giving travelers a way to spend less of their journey recovering from jet lag through a new partnership with Timeshifter, a circadian technology company whose jet lag app has been downloaded by more than 1.7 million users.</p>
<p>Effective immediately, travelers booking a Round the World trip through oneworld.com receive a complimentary Timeshifter subscription covering their entire itinerary, from departure through the final return. The personalized program takes into account each flight and time-zone change, along with the traveler’s typical sleep pattern, chronotype and preferences, to recommend when to seek or avoid light and when to sleep. With oneworld’s Round the World trips averaging seven stops and around three months in duration, the partnership addresses one of the less glamorous realities of extended global travel: staying in sync while the itinerary keeps moving.</p>
<h3>The Curated Take</h3>
<p>For travelers undertaking a true around-the-world journey, time is the luxury. A day spent foggy, exhausted or awake at the wrong hour is a day lost, making circadian planning an unexpectedly valuable addition to the premium travel experience. Rather than treating jet lag as an unavoidable consequence of long-haul travel, oneworld is building recovery and readiness into the journey itself.</p>
