<figure><img alt="" src="https://substack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com/public/images/8efd6adb-6a89-46ae-b0d0-502d0b9165eb_600x398.jpeg"/></figure><p>At Kandooma Maldives, the festive season takes on an adventurous spirit, with a Pirates’ Festive Adventure programme running from December 22, 2026, through January 7, 2027. Set on a private island in the South Malé Atoll, the resort brings together Christmas and New Year celebrations with the pleasures of a tropical escape, from treasure-inspired activities at Kandoo Kids’ Club and Santa’s arrival to shark and turtle snorkeling, shipwreck safaris, sunset cruises and surfing lessons. Festive dining adds a distinctly Maldivian note, with traditional boduberu drumming, fire dancing, a Christmas Eve gala and New Year celebrations. Guests can also retreat to Kandooma Spa by COMO Shambhala for restorative treatments, while the resort’s experience-led all-inclusive offering combines dining, family activities and opportunities to explore the surrounding Indian Ocean. The programme continues through Orthodox Christmas, giving the season an extended island celebration.</p><figure><img alt="" src="https://substack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com/public/images/0fa81f76-8bad-4f8c-b4ac-39ae46a44732_1200x500.jpeg"/><figcaption>Photo Credit: South Malé Atoll, Maldives. Courtesy of Kandooma Maldives.</figcaption></figure><h3>The Curated Take</h3><p>The Maldives is often associated with barefoot seclusion and overwater villas, but Kandooma offers a more active interpretation of the festive island escape. Its appeal lies in the range of experiences gathered into one setting: marine exploration for adventurous travelers, cultural performances and island flavors around the table, and dedicated activities that give families room to celebrate together. For those seeking a Christmas or New Year away from winter weather without sacrificing a sense of occasion, the combination of ocean adventure, wellness and Maldivian tradition makes for a spirited alternative to the usual holiday setting.</p>
