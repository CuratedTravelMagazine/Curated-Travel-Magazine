<p>New York City takes on a special kind of magic during the holidays, when festive music, seasonal treats, and beloved traditions transform Midtown into a winter wonderland. At InterContinental New York Barclay, the season brings classic New York celebrations together with thoughtful experiences for families and travelers of all ages. Thanksgiving and Christmas dining at The Parlour sets the scene for seasonal gatherings, while the annual Barclay Express: Cookies &amp; Cocoa invites families to decorate cookies and sip hot chocolate. Throughout the week of December 14, holiday choirs fill the lobby with music, and a postcard station offers guests the chance to send greetings to loved ones or Santa, whose replies may arrive at their guestrooms by way of the hotel’s elves. Younger guests can retreat to the Petit Chalet, a cozy A-frame hideaway stocked with blankets, pillows, and classic board games. The celebrations culminate on New Year’s Eve, when guests can welcome the year ahead at The Parlour while watching the iconic Times Square Ball Drop.</p>
<h3>The Curated Take</h3>
<p>Few cities make the holidays feel as cinematic as New York, where familiar rituals take on an extra sparkle against the energy of Midtown. From handwritten holiday greetings and carols in the lobby to watching the Times Square Ball Drop without stepping into the winter crowds, the Barclay’s seasonal experiences offer a more intimate way to enjoy the city’s festive spirit. For families and couples planning a New York holiday escape, it is an invitation to experience the season through small traditions, shared moments, and the unmistakable atmosphere of the city at its most celebratory.</p>
