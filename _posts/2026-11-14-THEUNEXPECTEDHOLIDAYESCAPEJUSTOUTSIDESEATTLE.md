<figure><img alt="" src="https://substack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com/public/images/4d01651c-3063-4e35-99c0-8bf0ac1c1257_600x684.jpeg"/></figure><p>Just minutes from Seattle-Tacoma International Airport, Seattle Southside is offering travelers and local visitors a variety of ways to make the holiday season part of the journey. From independent shops and locally made gifts to international markets and global dining, the region brings together experiences that extend well beyond an airport overnight. Visitors can browse specialty retailers, discover wildlife conservation gifts supporting Pacific Northwest marine rescue efforts, tour a chocolate factory or explore the area’s multicultural food scene. The destination is also highlighting its Stay, Play &amp; Fly approach, encouraging travelers to turn an early departure, overnight connection or extended layover into a mini getaway with nearby hotels, attractions and waterfront experiences. Seasonal highlights include Thanksgiving dining, wreathmaking workshops, Burien’s Holiday Cookie Crawl, the Argosy Christmas Ship Festival at Des Moines Marina and the Rainier Symphony’s holiday concert. New additions to the area include Molly Tea, West African restaurant Taste of Jollof and an expanded Interim Computer Museum, adding further reasons to explore Seattle Southside during the festive season.</p><figure><img alt="" src="https://substack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com/public/images/0fa81f76-8bad-4f8c-b4ac-39ae46a44732_1200x500.jpeg"/></figure><h3>The Curated Take</h3><p>Seattle Southside makes a compelling case for looking beyond the airport when traveling through the Seattle region. Its appeal lies in the combination of convenience and cultural variety: independent businesses, international food traditions, waterfront experiences and seasonal events create opportunities to turn otherwise overlooked travel time into something memorable. The emphasis on shopping small and discovering locally rooted businesses also gives the holiday experience a more personal dimension. For travelers passing through SEA, a little extra time here can offer a welcome introduction to the flavors and character of the Pacific Northwest.<br/><br/>Photo credit: Seattle Southside, Washington.
