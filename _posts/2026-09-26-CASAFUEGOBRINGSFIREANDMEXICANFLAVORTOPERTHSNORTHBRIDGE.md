<p>Perth’s Northbridge has a new destination for Mexican food and cocktails with the August opening of Casa Fuego. The restaurant and bar is built around the flavors and energy of Mexico, with a menu featuring birria beef tacos, tostadas and burritos made with hand-pressed tortillas, slow-cooked meats and house-made salsa. The drinks program adds a cocktail-driven element to the experience.</p>
<p>Located in one of Perth’s cultural and dining hubs, Casa Fuego adds another international influence to a neighborhood already known for its lively restaurant scene. The focus on handmade tortillas, slow cooking and bold flavors gives the concept a culinary identity beyond the familiar shorthand of Mexican-inspired dining.</p>
<p><strong>The Curated Take:</strong><br/>For travelers, the most interesting restaurant openings are often those that give a neighborhood another reason to explore it. Casa Fuego’s emphasis on handmade elements and fire-driven flavors makes it a natural addition to a Northbridge evening, particularly for visitors looking to experience Perth beyond its traditional sightseeing circuit.</p>
