The Waikoloa Beach Marriott Resort & Spa is entering a new chapter following a comprehensive renovation of its 297 guestrooms and suites, completed in December 2025. Inspired by Hawaiʻi Island’s natural landscape, the refreshed accommodations pair coastal colors, warm wood finishes and organic textures with cultural details. The resort has also introduced the Beach House at Waikoloa, a private oceanfront villa enclave offering dedicated concierge service, daily breakfast at Harvest 19 Kitchen & Bar and access to the wider resort’s dining, amenities and cultural programming. At Harvest 19, locally sourced ingredients celebrate the island’s agricultural heritage and the farmers, ranchers, fishermen and artisans who shape its food culture. Set along Anaehoʻomalu Bay, the resort brings together beachfront relaxation, contemporary accommodations and a stronger connection to the character of the Kohala Coast.

**The Curated Take**

A meaningful resort renovation should do more than update interiors; it should give travelers a deeper sense of where they are. Waikoloa Beach Marriott’s enhancements bring together three important elements of contemporary luxury hospitality: a renewed sense of place, more private accommodation options and dining rooted in local producers. The Beach House adds a residential-style experience for guests seeking greater privacy without giving up resort amenities, while Harvest 19 offers a connection to Hawaiʻi Island’s agricultural and culinary identity.
