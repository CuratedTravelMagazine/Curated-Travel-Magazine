<p>At Four Seasons Resort Maldives at Kuda Huraa, wellness is moving beyond the traditional spa experience with the debut of ŪRJĀ Naturopathy Island, a dedicated wellness destination centered on naturopathic care, personalized guidance and sustainable wellbeing. Named for the Sanskrit word for vitality, ŪRJĀ combines naturopathic principles with scientific expertise, with programs designed around four core pathways: Rest &amp; Rebalance, Cleanse, Nourish and Reconnect.</p>
<p>Reached by a traditional Maldivian dhoni from the resort’s main jetty, the island places wellness within a distinctly natural setting, with over-water treatment suites, open-air rituals and the surrounding ocean shaping the experience. Each journey begins with a naturopathic consultation, followed by individualized therapies, nutrition guidance and educational tools designed to help guests better understand their own wellbeing. Signature experiences include mud therapy, heliotherapy, sound healing, acupuncture and the Vagus Reset, while specialized programs address areas such as sleep, liver health and joint care. Yoga therapy is also offered as part of the program.</p>
<h3>The Curated Take</h3>
<p>ŪRJĀ is interesting because it treats a wellness retreat as an opportunity to learn, not simply unwind. The emphasis on consultation, education and practical habits gives the experience a more immersive dimension, while the private-island setting adds the kind of quiet and separation that can make meaningful restoration possible. For travelers increasingly looking for wellness experiences with a sense of purpose, the concept reflects a broader shift from the conventional spa escape toward more individualized approaches to wellbeing.</p>
