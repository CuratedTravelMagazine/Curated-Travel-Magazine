<figure><img alt="" src="https://substack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com/public/images/297cccaa-5a1a-4854-b418-dc9e0ab9a5fc_600x900.jpeg"/></figure><p>Pensacola, Florida, is preparing for its next chapter in destination development with the appointment of Tony Lyle as President and CEO of Visit Pensacola, effective October 26, 2026. Bringing more than 25 years of tourism and destination marketing experience, including his recent leadership of Visit Galveston in Texas, Lyle will guide the organization’s efforts to strengthen Pensacola’s position as a Gulf Coast travel destination. His background spans coastal tourism, international travel trade, leisure and group travel, sports tourism and destination brand development, with a focus on building partnerships and encouraging visitation beyond traditional peak travel periods. The Pensacola Bay Area offers a distinctive combination of more than 465 years of history, nearly 40 miles of sugar-white beaches and emerald Gulf waters, encompassing Pensacola, Pensacola Beach and Perdido Key. From its historic character as America’s First Settlement to its coastal appeal and evolving hospitality scene, the region brings together cultural discovery and relaxed beach escapes. Lyle’s appointment signals an opportunity to build on that identity while strengthening the partnerships and experiences that support tourism throughout Northwest Florida.</p><figure><img alt="" src="https://substack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com/public/images/0fa81f76-8bad-4f8c-b4ac-39ae46a44732_1200x500.jpeg"/><figcaption>Photo Credit: Pensacola, Florida. Courtesy of Visit Pensacola.</figcaption></figure><h3>The Curated Take</h3><p>A destination’s appeal is shaped by more than its beaches, hotels and attractions. The vision and partnerships behind its tourism industry also influence how a place evolves and what visitors discover when they arrive. Pensacola has a compelling foundation in its blend of Gulf Coast scenery and deep historical roots. As the destination looks toward its next chapter, the opportunity lies in bringing those elements together in ways that encourage travelers to explore beyond the shoreline and discover the broader character of the Pensacola Bay Area.</p>
