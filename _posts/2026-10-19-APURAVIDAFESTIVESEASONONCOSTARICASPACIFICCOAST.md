<p>On Costa Rica’s Pacific coast, the festive season takes on a sunlit character, with oceanfront celebrations, cacao traditions, and evenings beneath the stars offering an alternative to winter’s familiar rituals. Beginning in November 2027, Waldorf Astoria Costa Rica Punta Cacique will welcome the holidays with experiences that bring together traditional seasonal pleasures and the country’s <em>Pura Vida</em> spirit. Guests can take part in festive mixology classes, gather for stargazing and s’mores, explore the art of chocolate-making, and experience traditional cacao ceremonies. As the year draws to a close, Wishes by the Sea offers an oceanfront New Year’s Eve celebration centered on reflection, fresh beginnings, and the possibilities of the year ahead. For travelers seeking a luxury festive escape in Costa Rica, the program offers a warm-weather alternative where local traditions, natural surroundings, and meaningful moments shape the holiday experience.</p>
<h3>The Curated Take</h3>
<p>A change of scenery can also be a change of rhythm. Along Costa Rica’s Pacific coast, festive traditions move outdoors, from evenings spent beneath a star-filled sky to cacao experiences rooted in the region’s cultural heritage. The emphasis on reflection and new beginnings brings a thoughtful dimension to a tropical holiday escape, inviting travelers to welcome the year ahead with a renewed sense of connection to nature, place, and the people around them.</p>
