<p>Fusion is marking World Wellness Weekend, September 18–20, 2026, with a three-day program of complimentary wellness, outdoor and community experiences across its properties in Vietnam. From beachside tai chi and sound healing to herbal workshops, cycling, water sports, healthy cooking and environmental initiatives, the program reflects Fusion’s wellness-inclusive approach while extending beyond the traditional spa experience.</p>
<p>At <strong>Fusion Resort &amp; Villas Da Nang</strong>, guests can explore sunrise grounding, breathwork, mindful movement and sound-based experiences, while <strong>Alba Wellness Valley by Fusion</strong> combines meditation, tai chi, outdoor adventures, hot springs and a community charity initiative supporting the Hue Hope Center. <strong>Fusion Resort Cam Ranh</strong> adds tai chi, herbal and self-care workshops, a community plank challenge and a beach-cleaning initiative. At <strong>The Ocean Resort by Fusion Quy Nhon</strong>, tennis, kayaking, stand-up paddleboarding, cycling and complimentary water sports encourage guests to experience wellness through movement and the outdoors. In the cities, <strong>Grand Royal Riverside Hue - Fusion Collection</strong> offers healthy cooking, stretching and morning runs along the Perfume River, while <strong>Fusion Suites Vung Tau</strong> combines natural-ingredient workshops with charitable giving.</p>
<h3>The Curated Take</h3>
<p>What makes this program interesting is its breadth. Wellness here isn’t confined to a treatment room or a single type of practice. Guests can move, get outdoors, learn something practical, eat well, care for the environment or simply slow down, while several experiences also invite local communities to participate. For travelers who see wellbeing as part of how they experience a destination, rather than the sole purpose of a retreat, Fusion’s approach offers a more flexible take on the modern wellness getaway.</p>
