<figure><img alt="" src="https://substack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com/public/images/3ebf840d-b056-46b4-9ed1-93609fb0e9e6_600x450.png"/></figure><p>An Australian favorite gets a seasonal reinvention at Park Hyatt Sydney, where the hotel is bringing back its Tim Tam Afternoon Tea in collaboration with the iconic chocolate biscuit brand. Running from December 4, 2026, through February 28, 2027, the experience puts a distinctly Australian spin on the traditional afternoon tea, pairing familiar flavors with polished pastry craftsmanship and summer ingredients. Sweet selections include a mango and cream pavlova, an Iced Vovo-inspired éclair with raspberry and coconut, a chocolate and caramel biscuit creation, and a peach and rose roll. Savory offerings range from chicken and tarragon sandwiches to pork and fennel sausage rolls and chicken liver parfait brioche, alongside traditional scones and malt and vanilla cream beignets served with Tim Tam spread. Three festive drinks extend the collaboration, including a Tim Tam-inspired espresso martini and a non-alcoholic Iced Vovo mocktail. The result is a playful expression of Australian food culture in one of Sydney’s most celebrated luxury hotel settings.</p><figure><img alt="" src="https://substack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com/public/images/0fa81f76-8bad-4f8c-b4ac-39ae46a44732_1200x500.jpeg"/><figcaption>Photo Credit: Sydney, New South Wales, Australia. Courtesy of Park Hyatt Sydney.</figcaption></figure><h3>The Curated Take</h3><p>Afternoon tea has long been a ritual of elegant hotels, but its most memorable interpretations often reflect a sense of place. At Park Hyatt Sydney, the collaboration draws on a familiar Australian treat and gives it a new context through seasonal ingredients, inventive pastry and a distinctly local sense of fun. For international visitors, it offers a delicious introduction to a homegrown favorite; for Sydney locals, a reason to revisit a classic hotel ritual with a fresh perspective. Sometimes, a destination’s character is best discovered through something as simple as what is served at the table.</p>
