At InterContinental Dominica Cabrits Resort & Spa, an excursion to the Kalinago Territory offers travelers an opportunity to encounter one of the Caribbean’s enduring Indigenous communities through its living traditions. Located along Dominica’s northeastern coast, the territory spans 3,700 acres across eight hamlets and is home to approximately 3,000 Kalinago people. Rather than approaching Indigenous heritage solely through historical accounts, the experience introduces visitors to the community’s contemporary cultural life through traditional music, dance, and storytelling, alongside generations-old artisanal practices such as basket weaving, canoe building, pottery, and wood carving. The encounter provides a window into the skills, knowledge, and customs that continue to shape Kalinago identity, offering a deeper understanding of Dominica beyond its celebrated rainforests, volcanic landscapes, and natural attractions.

### The Curated Take

The most meaningful cultural encounters do more than preserve the past as something to observe. They create opportunities to recognize living communities, their knowledge, and the traditions that continue to shape their identity. The Kalinago Territory offers a perspective on Dominica that cannot be found in its scenery alone, placing the island’s Indigenous heritage and the people who carry it forward at the center of the experience.
