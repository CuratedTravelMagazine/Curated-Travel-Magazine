<p>Venice Carnival takes on a more intimate character at Violino d’Oro, where guests can experience one of Italy’s most celebrated traditions through local craftsmanship, private access and insider connections. Located in the heart of Venice, the hotel is offering a Carnival experience from January 30 through February 9 that begins with a visit to a Venetian atelier and a meeting with local designer Antonia Sautter. Guests can learn about the artistry behind the costumes before selecting a customized, made-to-measure Carnival dress, with Violino d’Oro arranging the fittings, water taxi transfers and access to one of Venice’s prestigious themed Carnival balls.</p>
<p>The experience continues beyond the costume with private entry to Palazzo Ducale and the prisons associated with Casanova’s famous escape, tea at the historic Caffè Florian, and a masked photoshoot through Venice’s canals aboard a gondola. Guests can also take part in a cocktail class at Il Piccolo Bar, creating an itinerary that connects the spectacle of Carnival with the craftsmanship, history and traditions of Venice itself. Rather than simply attending Carnival, the experience offers travelers the opportunity to encounter the festival through the people and places that give it its distinctive character.</p>
<p><strong>The Curated Take:</strong> What makes this experience compelling is the access behind the spectacle. Carnival is one of Venice’s most recognizable traditions, but Violino d’Oro builds an itinerary around the creative process and local relationships that exist beneath the masks and costumes. Meeting a Venetian designer, commissioning a personalized look and gaining access to historic and cultural sites creates a more layered way to experience Venice during Carnival, particularly for luxury travelers who value craftsmanship, storytelling and connections with the destination.</p>
