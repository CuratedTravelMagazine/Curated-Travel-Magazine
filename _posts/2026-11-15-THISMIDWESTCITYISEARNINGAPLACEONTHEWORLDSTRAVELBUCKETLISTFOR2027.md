<figure><img alt="" src="https://substack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com/public/images/c109f328-bba0-498c-827d-2b370a837499_1440x1080.jpeg"/></figure><p>Madison, Wisconsin, is stepping into the international travel spotlight after being named one of National Geographic’s Best of the World destinations for 2027, joining a global collection of cultural landmarks, outdoor escapes and emerging travel experiences. Wisconsin’s capital offers a distinctive blend of urban outdoor adventure, culinary discovery and creative culture, with its location between lakes Mendota and Monona shaping much of the city’s character. More than 200 miles of bicycle paths, abundant parks and accessible waterfront spaces make it easy to balance city exploration with time outdoors, while the historic Dane County Farmers’ Market, one of the largest producers-only farmers’ markets in the United States, offers a taste of the region’s agricultural and culinary traditions. Madison’s food scene has also earned national recognition, with 47 James Beard Award nominations to date and a growing collection of restaurants, bars and neighborhood gathering places. Cultural highlights include Olbrich Botanical Gardens, live music venues, independent businesses and the forthcoming Madison Public Market. For travelers seeking an American city break with a strong sense of place, Madison brings together lakefront scenery, farm-to-table dining, sustainability and a creative local identity in a refreshingly approachable Midwestern destination.</p><figure><img alt="" src="https://substack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com/public/images/0fa81f76-8bad-4f8c-b4ac-39ae46a44732_1200x500.jpeg"/><figcaption>Photo Credit: Madison, Wisconsin. Courtesy of Destination Madison.</figcaption></figure><h3>The Curated Take</h3><p>Madison’s recognition is a reminder that compelling travel experiences are not always found in the world’s most obvious destinations. Its appeal comes from the relationship between the city and its natural surroundings, where lakeside recreation, farmers’ markets, culinary traditions and cultural spaces are woven into everyday life. For travelers interested in exploring beyond the familiar American city escapes, Madison offers an opportunity to experience the Midwest through its food, outdoor culture and independent spirit. Its National Geographic recognition makes 2027 an especially timely year to discover what gives this lakeside capital its character.</p>
