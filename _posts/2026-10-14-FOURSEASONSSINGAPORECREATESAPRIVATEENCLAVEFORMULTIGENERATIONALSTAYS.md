<p>Four Seasons Hotel Singapore has introduced a new Three-Bedroom Premier Suite designed to give families and friends the ability to travel together without giving up personal space. Set along tree-lined Orchard Boulevard, the 180-square-metre residential-style suite has its own private entrance and discreet corridor that can be closed off to connect the Premier Suite with two additional guest rooms, creating a private three-bedroom enclave. Separate bedrooms and marble bathrooms provide individual retreat spaces, while a generous living area, private bar and flexible dining and workspace create room for everyone to gather.</p>
<p>The suite is designed particularly with multigenerational holidays, celebrations and longer stays in mind, complementing Four Seasons Singapore’s broader collection of 41 suites. Guests can move from their private enclave to the hotel’s dining and wellness offerings, including Cantonese cuisine at Jiang-Nan Chun, Japanese-Peruvian dining at Nobu Singapore and botanical-inspired experiences at One-Ninety. The Four Seasons Spa and Chi Longevity Clinic add further options for relaxation and personalized wellbeing, while the hotel’s location places guests near Orchard Road and Singapore Botanic Gardens.</p>
<p><strong>The Curated Take:</strong> The suite reflects a shift in how luxury families are traveling. The goal is no longer simply to provide a larger room, but to create a private residential environment that allows different generations or groups of friends to share a trip while maintaining independence. That combination of togetherness and personal space is particularly relevant to longer, experience-led stays where the accommodation becomes part of the destination experience.</p>
