<p>For travelers who would rather lean into winter than escape it, Mountain View Grand Resort &amp; Spa offers a classic White Mountains retreat in New Hampshire. Set against the mountain landscape, the historic resort provides a home base for embracing the season, with skiing, snowshoeing, winter hiking and snowy mountain scenery all close at hand.</p>
<p>Back at the resort, the experience shifts from outdoor adventure to cozy restoration. Guests can warm up in the indoor pool, retreat to the Tower Spa or settle into fireside spaces after a day in the cold. The combination makes winter itself part of the appeal, rather than an obstacle to a getaway, giving travelers the chance to move between the energy of the mountains and the comfort of a traditional New England resort.</p>
<p><strong>The Curated Take:</strong> There is something increasingly appealing about destinations that don’t try to neutralize winter. Mountain View Grand embraces the season as a defining part of the experience, offering the kind of snowy escape where cold-weather adventure and fireside relaxation are meant to complement one another.</p>
