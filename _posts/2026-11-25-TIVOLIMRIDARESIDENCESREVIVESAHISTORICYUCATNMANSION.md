Tivoli Mérida Residences will introduce 69 branded residences in the Yucatán capital, with the historic Casona 333 serving as the property’s social center. Its original character will frame a collection of guest amenities, including a restaurant, boulangerie, bar and piano lounge, along with a garden and terrace designed for private gatherings. The project brings Tivoli’s Latin American expansion to one of Mexico’s most culturally distinctive cities.</p><figure><img alt="" src="https://substack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com/public/images/0fa81f76-8bad-4f8c-b4ac-39ae46a44732_1200x500.jpeg"/><figcaption>Photo Credit: Courtesy of Minor Hotels.</figcaption></figure><p><strong>The Curated Take</strong></p><p>The appeal here is the balance between the independence of a residence and the atmosphere of a historic hotel. In a city where architecture, food and local culture are central to the experience, placing a restored mansion at the center of the property gives the development a meaningful sense of place.</p>
