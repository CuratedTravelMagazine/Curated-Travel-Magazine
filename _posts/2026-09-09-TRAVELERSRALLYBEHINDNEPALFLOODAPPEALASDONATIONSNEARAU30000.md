<p>Within hours of launching an emergency appeal for communities affected by devastating flooding in Nepal’s Rasuwa district near the Tibet border, the <strong>World Expeditions Foundation</strong> was approaching its AU$30,000 fundraising target. The response highlights the deep connection many travelers have with Nepal, a country where World Expeditions has operated for decades and built long-standing relationships with local communities, guides and partners. The company has also confirmed that all World Expeditions clients and staff currently traveling in Nepal are safe.</p>
<p>The foundation is directing donations toward communities affected by the flash flood and will continue to share updates as recovery efforts unfold. For travelers who have experienced Nepal firsthand, the appeal offers an opportunity to support communities that have long welcomed visitors to the country’s trekking and mountaineering regions.</p>
<p><strong>The Curated Take:</strong> Travel creates connections that often extend well beyond a trip. The rapid response to this appeal is a reminder that the relationship between travelers and destinations can become especially meaningful when communities face a crisis. For those who know Nepal through its landscapes, culture and people, this is a chance to give something back.</p>
