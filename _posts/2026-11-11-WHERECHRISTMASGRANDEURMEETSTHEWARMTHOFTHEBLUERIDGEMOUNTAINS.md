<figure><img alt="" src="https://substack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com/public/images/628d132e-e900-4dca-b51e-0ae802c94200_600x400.jpeg"/></figure><p>The Blue Ridge Mountains provide a memorable setting for a holiday escape, and Asheville, North Carolina, is welcoming the 2026 festive season with a mix of grand traditions, mountain-town celebrations, culinary experiences and creative discoveries. The season’s signature attractions include the 131st Christmas at Biltmore, with its illuminated estate and candlelit evenings, Winter Lights at the North Carolina Arboretum, and the National Gingerbread House Competition at Omni Grove Park Inn. Beyond the familiar celebrations, visitors can discover a more contemporary side of the holidays through Warren Haynes’s Christmas Jam, local artisan markets, hands-on glassblowing and jewelry-making workshops, and seasonal dining across the city. Families can enjoy performances of The Nutcracker and Frozen, while couples and wellness-minded travelers can retreat to mountain-view saunas, historic hotel fireplaces and festive rooftop lounges. With celebrations extending into January, Asheville also makes an appealing destination for a post-Christmas getaway, combining the warmth of Appalachian holiday traditions with the region’s distinctive arts, food and hospitality.</p><figure><img alt="" src="https://substack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com/public/images/0fa81f76-8bad-4f8c-b4ac-39ae46a44732_1200x500.jpeg"/><figcaption>Photo Credit: Asheville, North Carolina, USA. Courtesy of Explore Asheville.</figcaption></figure><h3>The Curated Take</h3><p>The most memorable holiday destinations offer more than a calendar of seasonal events. They create an atmosphere that invites travelers to slow down, reconnect and discover something unexpected. Asheville brings together the nostalgia of a grand estate dressed for Christmas, the creative energy of an independent arts community and the natural beauty of the Blue Ridge Mountains. Its appeal lies in that balance: a destination where a festive getaway can be as lively or restorative as the traveler chooses, with enough variety to make a multigenerational holiday feel personal rather than prescribed.</p>
