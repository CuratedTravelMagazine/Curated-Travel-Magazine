<p>Muscat is gaining a new luxury address with the opening of Tivoli LA VIE Muscat Hotel &amp; Residences, a 79-room property that blends Tivoli’s European heritage with contemporary design and subtle references to Omani craftsmanship. Located within the prestigious LA VIE development in Madinat Al Irfan, the hotel places travelers near Muscat’s cultural and entertainment attractions while offering an experience designed around golf, gastronomy and wellbeing.</p>
<p>Guest rooms and suites feature natural textures, coastal tones, private balconies and views across the championship golf course, with details ranging from Amouage bath amenities to Dyson technology and Läderach chocolate at turndown. Dining is a major part of the property’s identity, from Mediterranean cuisine at S O L É to the rooftop SEEN by Olivier da Costa, the concept’s first Middle Eastern outpost. Nineteenth Sports Bar and Cigar Vault adds a more relaxed evening option, while Benihana and The Coffee Club are set to join the culinary lineup later this year.</p>
<p>Wellness is equally central, with Muscat’s first Anantara Spa, a rooftop infinity pool and Technogym fitness facilities. Golfers can play the Paul Thomas-designed 18-hole course or use the country’s only Toptracer facility. The property also positions itself for major events, with the 520-guest Juman Ballroom overlooking the golf course.</p>
<p><strong>The Curated Take:</strong> Tivoli LA VIE is interesting not simply because it is new, but because of how deliberately it connects several facets of contemporary luxury travel in Oman. European hospitality, Omani design influences, serious golf, destination dining and a substantial wellness offering come together within a single address. For travelers looking beyond the region’s more established luxury destinations, the opening reflects Muscat’s growing ability to offer a sophisticated stay without losing its sense of place.</p>
