<p>The St. Regis Maldives Vommuli Resort will mark its 10th anniversary this festive season by turning the story of Vommuli Island into the foundation for its celebrations. From December 20, 2026 through January 7, 2027, “Chapter X” will explore the island through four distinct ecological zones: Lagoon, Beach, Jungle and Coast. Each becomes a setting for festive dining, cultural experiences, wellness, family activities and St. Regis traditions, creating a celebration that looks beyond the resort’s anniversary to the natural and cultural character of its Maldivian setting.</p>
<p>The Lagoon zone draws on the surrounding marine life and overwater architecture, while the Beach zone reflects the island’s coastal heritage and connection to generations of Maldivian fishing traditions. In the Jungle, Vommuli House becomes a space for children and multigenerational families to explore local biodiversity through storytelling and nature-focused activities. The Coastal zone takes inspiration from the traditional dhoni and the Maldives’ seafaring heritage. The festive calendar will also include Christmas traditions, culinary events, wellness experiences and a New Year’s Eve celebration overlooking the Indian Ocean.</p>
<p><strong>The Curated Take:</strong> An anniversary can easily become an exercise in looking backward. At Vommuli, the more interesting idea is to use 10 years as a reason to look more closely at the island itself. By organizing the festive season around its different environments and layers of heritage, the resort makes its milestone feel connected to place rather than simply to the passage of time.</p>
