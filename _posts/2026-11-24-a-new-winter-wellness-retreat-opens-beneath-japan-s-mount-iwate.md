Set against the snow-covered landscape of northern Japan, **Azuma Farm Koiwai** is welcoming its first winter season with the introduction of natural hot springs and Forest Springs SPA treatments. Opening November 20, the mineral-rich waters are drawn from 1,000 meters underground and supplied directly to the private baths in all 24 guest villas and the property’s three forest sauna pavilions. Beginning December 1, guests can also experience personalized massage treatments inspired by nature and the Five Elements, including a signature ritual combining horsehair brushing, warm stones and hands-on massage. Winter experiences extend beyond the spa, with guided snowshoe walks through forests and pastures beneath Mount Iwate and access to skiing at nearby Appi Kogen.

**The Curated Take**

Japan’s onsen culture has long been central to the art of the winter retreat. Azuma Farm Koiwai brings that tradition into a private, nature-led setting, pairing the restorative appeal of natural hot springs with forest saunas, wellness treatments and outdoor exploration. It offers travelers a compelling reason to look beyond Japan’s familiar urban and resort destinations and discover the quieter winter landscapes of Tohoku.
