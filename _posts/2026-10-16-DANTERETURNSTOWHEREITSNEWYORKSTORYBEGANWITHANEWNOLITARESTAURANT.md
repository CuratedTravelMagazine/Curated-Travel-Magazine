<p>Dante is returning to the place where its New York story began. This fall, Australian husband-and-wife hospitality duo Linden Pride and Nathalie Hudson will open Dante Elizabeth Street at 210 Elizabeth Street in Manhattan’s Nolita, the same address where the couple once decided to build their life and hospitality career in New York. The new restaurant brings together two defining elements of the Dante identity: a wood-fired grill and oven led by Executive Chef Daniel Garwood and an aperitivo bar built around the Italian ritual of lingering over drinks and conversation. The result is intended as a distinctly New York expression of the brand’s evolution across New York, Los Angeles and London.</p>
<p>Garwood’s cooking draws on his experience in Tasmania, Italy and Australia, with the open fire at the center of an ingredient-led menu designed for sharing. On the other side of the room, Pride’s cocktail program puts particular attention on the Spritz, alongside Martinis, Negronis and reinterpretations of classic cocktails. The 205-seat space, designed by Nathalie Hudson with AvroKO’s Adam Farmerie, combines a horseshoe-shaped bar, open kitchen, layered lighting and eclectic artwork, while the restaurant’s Elizabeth Street setting places it within one of downtown Manhattan’s more intimate crossroads, near the Elizabeth Street Garden, SoHo, the Lower East Side and East Village.</p>
<p><strong>The Curated Take:</strong> The most compelling part of Dante Elizabeth Street is not simply that an established restaurant brand is opening another New York location. It is that Pride and Hudson are returning to the exact place where they made the decision that set their hospitality story in motion. The new restaurant therefore becomes both a homecoming and a statement about what Dante has become: an Italian-inspired New York institution that has spent a decade refining the art of the aperitivo while continuing to evolve.</p>
