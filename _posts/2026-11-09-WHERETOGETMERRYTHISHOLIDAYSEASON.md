Courtesy of Burg Hotel Lech.</figcaption></figure><p>The holiday season has a way of bringing familiar traditions back into focus, but there’s something special about experiencing them somewhere new. Perhaps it’s a Christmas tree lighting in a mountain village, an evening spent wandering beneath illuminated Alpine facades, or the excitement of waking up on Christmas morning in a centuries-old Irish castle. Across the U.S. and Europe, hotels and resorts are finding their own ways to celebrate, pairing seasonal traditions with distinctive experiences that make the holidays worth traveling for.</p><p>Whether the plan is a family escape, a romantic winter retreat, or simply an excuse to extend the celebrations beyond home, here are a few places to get merry this holiday season.</p><h2>A Season of Lights in the Swiss Alps</h2><p>In the Swiss Alpine village of Évolène, the holiday season brings a particularly enchanting reason to step outside after dark. From December 6 through January 6, <em>Évolène en Lumière</em> transforms Rue Centrale into an illuminated celebration of local heritage. Each evening from 5 p.m. to 10 p.m., light and music projections bring the historic village facades, including the church, to life. Lighting installations throughout the village tell stories drawn from regional traditions and local mythology, creating an immersive walk through the area’s cultural history.</p><p>Guests staying at <a href="https://www.rucher.com/">Le Rucher d’Évolène</a> can also enjoy the seasonal atmosphere surrounding the new Hôtel Le Central, where a charming Christmas market takes place on the church square. With the market directly in front of the hotel, the village’s festive traditions are never far away.</p><figure><img alt="" src="https://substack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com/public/images/0fa81f76-8bad-4f8c-b4ac-39ae46a44732_1200x500.jpeg"/></figure><h2>A Handmade Christmas Above Lake Lucerne</h2><p>A little higher above the water, <a href="https://burgenstockresort.com/en/seasons">Bürgenstock Resort</a> celebrates the season with <em>A Handmade Christmas</em>, a December program centered on craftsmanship, creativity, and time together. Rather than simply decorating for the holidays, the resort invites guests to take part in them through wreath-making, pottery, painting, baking workshops, and miniature Christmas tree decorating.</p><p>The experience extends outdoors, too. Torchlit hikes accompanied by Glühwein and roasted chestnuts offer a distinctly Alpine way to spend a winter evening, while families can look forward to Christmas films, craft activities, outdoor adventures, and a visit from Santa on Christmas Eve.</p><p>For those looking to make the occasion a longer escape, two festive packages offer different interpretations of a Swiss Christmas. <em>Legendary Christmas</em> includes a decorated in-suite Christmas tree, a champagne welcome, and a private cinema experience complete with Christmas cookies, popcorn, and hot chocolate. <em>A Winter Getaway</em> takes a more adventurous approach, bringing guests into the mountains by 4x4 or snowmobile before a snowshoe journey through snow-covered forests to a local farm for lunch with a farming family. Both packages include breakfast, Alpine Spa access, and use of the resort’s funicular and catamaran.</p><p>As the year draws to a close, five distinct dining experiences across the mountain provide options for New Year’s Eve, with menus ranging from contemporary Asian and Persian cuisine to traditional Swiss Fondue Chinoise.</p><h2>A White Christmas in the Austrian Alps</h2><p>In Oberlech, Austria, where the village is car-free, Christmas at <a href="https://www.burghotel-lech.com/en/">Burg Hotel Lech</a> is shaped by the traditions of a family-run Alpine retreat. Christmas Eve begins with carols in the lobby, led by the Lucian family and accompanied by the Mozarteum Salzburg String Quartet. Santa Claus makes an appearance with gifts for younger guests, while the quartet continues playing through Christmas dinner.</p><p>The festivities extend throughout Advent with surprises waiting behind the doors of the hotel’s Burg Advent Calendar. And when Christmas gives way to the new year, the celebrations continue with a New Year’s Eve party and live DJ, welcoming 2027 in distinctly festive fashion.</p><h2>Winter Evenings at Forsthofgut Nature Hotel</h2><p>At <a href="https://www.forsthofgut.at/en/">Forsthofgut Nature Hotel</a> in Leogang, Austria, winter is an invitation to slow down and settle into the pleasures of the season. A new outdoor ice rink overlooking the Leoganger Steinberge offers a scenic setting for skating, while the slopeside setting of Restaurant 1617 brings guests closer to the mountain atmosphere.</p><p>After time on the ice, guests can warm up with mulled wine, hot chocolate, and roasted chestnuts before gathering around the fireplace for Austrian specialties and fondue. Cheese, meat, and chocolate variations are served in wood-paneled dining rooms warmed by tiled stoves and open fireplaces.</p><p>The hotel’s <em>Looking Forward to Christmas</em> package adds a seasonal touch to a winter stay, with homemade Christmas cookies and a spa treatment. Available November 28 through December 19, the package offers three- to seven-night stays.</p><h2>Holiday Traditions and a Little Christmas Magic in Michigan</h2><p>Not every holiday escape requires a trip across the Atlantic. At <a href="https://www.saintjohnsresort.com/detroit-hotel-deals">Saint John’s Resort</a> in Michigan, the season unfolds with a lineup of celebrations designed to bring families together.</p><p>The festivities begin with a Thanksgiving feast on November 26 in the resort’s Monarch Ballroom, where guests can enjoy traditional holiday favorites alongside more indulgent offerings, including corn-manchego pudding, sourdough bread stuffing, turkey and prime rib carving stations, and an interactive pie and cheesecake station.</p><p>The following evening, November 27, the Monarch Courtyard hosts the resort’s Christmas tree lighting. Thousands of twinkling lights, live carolers, hot cocoa, and a gathering around the fire set the tone for the weeks ahead.</p><p>Families with younger children may especially enjoy the resort’s <em>A Very Merry Holiday Sleepover Package</em>, available December 4, 11, and 18. The evening begins with an in-room visit from one of Santa’s elves, complete with a children’s book and sweet treat before bedtime. The following morning brings breakfast at FIVE Steakhouse, accompanied by a $100 dining credit, and a special visit from Santa.</p><p>For those who prefer to save their celebration for the final night of the year, The Wine Grotto hosts a New Year’s Eve gathering with an expansive buffet featuring charcuterie, fresh seafood, and a New York strip carving station, alongside music and an open bar.</p><h2>A Winter Wonderland in Stowe, Vermont</h2><p>At the base of Mount Mansfield, <a href="https://www.sprucepeak.com/stay/the-lodge/">The Lodge at Spruce Peak</a> brings together the pleasures of a mountain winter and the traditions of the holiday season. As Stowe Mountain Resort opens for skiing in November, guests have direct access to the slopes, along with the restaurants, outdoor activities, and village celebrations that make Spruce Peak a destination in its own right.</p><p>The holiday calendar begins with Thanksgiving dinner on November 26, followed by the return of Mistletoe’s Holiday Bar on November 27. Throughout December, the resort adds seasonal touches to the experience, from Saturday ramen lunches at Tipsy Trout to the Holiday Retreat Weekend on December 4 and 5, featuring culinary experiences and festive activities.</p><p>Families can join the Holiday Gnome Hunt beginning December 18, following clues throughout Spruce Peak Village in search of hidden gnomes and a prize. The annual Spruce Peak Lights Festival follows on December 19, bringing Ice Dance International’s <em>Holiday on Ice</em>, photos with Santa, and additional village celebrations.</p><p>Christmas week includes afternoon tea on Christmas Eve and a Christmas Day dinner buffet. The celebrations continue on December 26 with an Oyster Takeover and a Holiday Hits Glow Skate Party at the resort’s nearly 10,000-square-foot outdoor ice rink.</p><p>For New Year’s Eve, the festivities extend across the mountain, with dinner and a countdown party at The Lodge, followed by the village’s <em>Fire &amp; Ice</em> celebration, featuring Ice Dance International and fireworks over the mountain. Between skiing, skating, snowshoeing, Nordic skiing, and warming up with a whiskey cocktail or raclette at the WhistlePig Pavilion, there is plenty to keep winter visitors entertained well beyond the holiday table.</p><h2>An Irish Christmas at Lough Eske Castle</h2><p>In Donegal, Ireland, <a href="https://www.lougheskecastlehotel.com/">Lough Eske Castle</a> offers a Christmas rooted in the traditions of a grand 19th-century castle. The four-night festive program, running December 23 through 26, brings together seasonal dining, live music, family activities, and the familiar rituals that make Christmas feel like Christmas.</p><p>Guests arrive on December 23 before the celebrations begin on Christmas Eve with a three-course dinner at Cedars Restaurant or the Gallery Bar, followed by mulled wine and mince pies in the castle’s main foyer. Live music in the Gallery Bar sets the tone for the evening.</p><p>Christmas Day begins with a full Irish breakfast in Cedars Restaurant, followed by a festive buffet lunch in the Castle Ballroom. The evening continues with a Christmas Night Buffet Supper in the Gallery Bar, accompanied by live entertainment. On December 26, St. Stephen’s Day, guests can enjoy another leisurely breakfast, time to explore the castle grounds, a three-course dinner, and an additional evening of music.</p><p>Younger guests have their own celebrations, including <em>Santa’s Little Helpers</em> activities on Christmas Eve, a visit from Santa on Christmas Day, a festive fairy trail, and seasonal activities throughout the stay. Earlier in December, the castle also hosts its Children’s Teddy Bear Afternoon Tea Party, December 13 through 20, combining festive treats with a Christmas disco and a special visit from Santa.</p><p>The season officially begins November 29 with the castle’s annual Christmas Lighting Ceremony. Alongside the lighting, children can enjoy a festive disco in the Grand Ballroom. The event also launches the castle’s annual St. Vincent de Paul Christmas charity food drive, supporting local families. It’s a reminder that some of the most meaningful holiday traditions extend beyond the guests staying within the castle walls.</p><figure><img alt="" src="https://substack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com/public/images/80809730-f4e0-4361-8512-6922eeee3734_1545x2000.jpeg"/></figure><p>This piece is part of the latest issue of <strong>Curated Travel Magazine</strong>, where we explore travel through immersive stories, cultural insight, and thoughtfully curated experiences around the world.</p><p>Enjoyed this article?
