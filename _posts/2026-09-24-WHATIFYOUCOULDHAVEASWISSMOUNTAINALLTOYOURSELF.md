<p>High above Lake St. Moritz in St. Moritz, Switzerland, the five-star, all-suite Carlton Hotel St. Moritz offers a winter escape built around both the glamour and the quieter pleasures of the Engadine. Guests can explore the snowy landscape by horse-drawn carriage, husky sled, ice skate across the frozen lake or venture out with the hotel’s Outdoor Butler, who can arrange everything from a personalized ski tour to a snow trek through the surrounding mountains. The resort’s setting also puts travelers within easy reach of the designer boutiques and polished atmosphere of St. Moritz, one of Switzerland’s most renowned winter destinations.</p>
<p>For an experience that takes exclusivity a step further, the hotel’s Rent a Mountain experience gives guests private access to an illuminated ski slope and gondola for an evening on the mountain without the usual crowds. Back at the hotel, the experience can continue with a private Fondue Gondola or time spent unwinding at the Bel Etage. It is a distinctly St. Moritz approach to winter travel: alpine adventure, après-ski indulgence and the kind of privacy that turns a snowy escape into something truly memorable.</p>
<p><strong>The Curated Take:</strong> The appeal of Carlton Hotel St. Moritz is not simply its access to winter sports, but the range of ways it allows travelers to experience the Swiss Alps on their own terms. The combination of a personal Outdoor Butler, private mountain access and refined après-ski experiences creates a version of St. Moritz that feels less like checking off a list of winter activities and more like having the landscape arranged around you. For luxury travelers seeking both iconic Alpine scenery and meaningful exclusivity, that distinction is significant.</p>
