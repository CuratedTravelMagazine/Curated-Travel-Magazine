<p>Winter in New York City gets a cozy, theatrical makeover at Kimpton Hotel Eventi, where the Winter Lodge Suite transforms a Manhattan stay into an immersive seasonal escape. Set at the crossroads of NoMad and Chelsea, the one- and two-bedroom suites are dressed in flocked décor, faux eucalyptus and fir garlands, silver accents and other winter-inspired details, creating the feel of a private alpine retreat without leaving the city.</p>
<p>The experience extends beyond the suite with coordinated winter pajamas for guests and furry companions, ice skating tickets and rentals, an après-skate amenity and a complimentary round of winter drinks at Eventi’s Back Bar. Accommodating groups of up to six or eight depending on the suite, the concept offers a particularly playful way to experience New York during the colder months, pairing the energy of Manhattan with the comforts and rituals of a winter lodge.</p>
<p><strong>The Curated Take:</strong> There is something appealing about treating winter in New York as a destination experience rather than something to escape. The Winter Lodge Suite leans fully into the season, creating an intimate, nostalgic counterpoint to the city outside and making the hotel itself part of the winter itinerary.</p>
