<p>On the quieter west coast of Koh Chang, Thailand, <strong>The Retreat Koh Chang</strong> has earned its first MICHELIN One Key in the 2026 MICHELIN Guide Hotel Selection. But the more revealing story may be the family behind the 38-room boutique resort, where siblings Mook and Mate Teeraniti are bringing a new generation of Thai hospitality to an island better known for its beaches and tropical scenery.</p>
<p>Surrounded by forest and overlooking the Gulf of Thailand, The Retreat is locally owned and operated, with the Teeraniti siblings taking an active role in shaping the guest experience. Their approach combines the intimacy of a small property with wellness, personalized experiences and opportunities to explore Koh Chang beyond the traditional beach-resort experience. The resort includes rooms, suites and a private villa, along with an infinity pool and experiences centered on the island’s natural environment. It is also a member of Small Luxury Hotels of the World.</p>
<p><strong>The Curated Take:</strong> The Retreat offers a window into how luxury hospitality in Southeast Asia is evolving through local ownership and a younger generation of hoteliers. Rather than separating the resort from its surroundings, Mook and Mate are building the property around their connection to Koh Chang and its community. The MICHELIN One Key provides the recognition, but the more compelling story is how a family-run resort is translating local knowledge and heritage into a contemporary luxury experience.</p>
