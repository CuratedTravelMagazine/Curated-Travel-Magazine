<p>A new 13-day itinerary from Bali Moto Adventures offers a different way to experience Indonesia’s extraordinary geography, linking seven islands across three archipelagos by motorcycle and boat. Beginning in Bali, the journey moves through Mount Batur, Ubud and lesser-known landscapes, with routes that pass volcanic terrain, rice terraces, waterfalls and temples before heading offshore to the Gili Islands.</p>
<p>The experience shifts with each island. In the Gilis, where motorized vehicles are prohibited, travelers explore by bicycle, traditional horse-drawn carriage and on the water, with snorkeling and diving included. From there, the journey continues to Nusa Penida, with stops at Kelingking Beach, Broken Beach and Diamond Beach, before reaching Nusa Lembongan and Nusa Ceningan via the Yellow Bridge. Rather than treating the islands as separate destinations, the itinerary makes the connections between them part of the adventure, combining riding, boat travel and time on the water.</p>
<h3>The Curated Take</h3>
<p>What makes this itinerary compelling is its sense of movement. The motorcycle provides access to Indonesia’s inland landscapes, while the crossings between islands reveal how dramatically the scenery and pace can change. For travelers who want more than a resort-based Bali experience, it offers a road-trip approach to discovering the archipelago, with each island becoming part of a larger journey.</p>
