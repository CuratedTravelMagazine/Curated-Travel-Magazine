<p>Lemala Kuria Hills Lodge in Tanzania’s Northern Serengeti has completed a renovation designed to enhance the guest experience without expanding beyond its established wilderness footprint. The refresh introduces a new two-bedroom family suite with generous living space and a private plunge pool, relocates the spa and redesigns and expands the main dining tent. Set among the granite boulders and kopjes of Wogakuria Hill near the Mara River, the lodge remains ideally positioned for the Great Migration while offering year-round wildlife viewing.</p>
<p>The new family suite adds flexibility for multigenerational travelers and groups, while the redesigned dining and spa spaces strengthen the lodge’s connection to its surrounding landscape. Guests can also explore the Northern Serengeti by guided game drive, walking safari or hot-air balloon, with the region’s relatively low visitor density offering a more intimate wilderness experience.</p>
<p><strong>The Curated Take:</strong> The renovation is notable for what it does not do. Rather than adding more structures to the landscape, Lemala has reworked its existing footprint to create a more flexible, family-friendly experience while preserving the sense of place that makes a remote Serengeti camp special. For travelers seeking safari with both comfort and a lighter touch on the wilderness, that distinction matters.</p>
