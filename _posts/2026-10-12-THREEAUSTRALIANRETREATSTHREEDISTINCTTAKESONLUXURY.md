<p>Three hotels within Salter Brothers Hospitality’s Worlds Apart collection have earned recognition in the 2026 MICHELIN Key hotel selection, illustrating just how differently luxury can take shape across Australia. Newly relaunched Ardour Milton Park Bowral in the Southern Highlands received One MICHELIN Key, while Spicers Peak Lodge in Queensland’s Scenic Rim and Spicers Sangoma Retreat in the Blue Mountains retained Two Keys. The recognition comes as MICHELIN’s hotel guide continues to expand its presence across Australia, where properties are assessed for everything from design and service to character and connection to place.</p>
<p>The three retreats offer distinctly different experiences. Ardour Milton Park Bowral combines the architecture and gardens of a historic Southern Highlands estate with contemporary design, destination dining and wellness following its 2026 transformation. In Queensland, Spicers Peak Lodge pairs an intimate, all-inclusive experience with personalized service and the wilderness of the Great Dividing Range, while adults-only Spicers Sangoma Retreat offers secluded bushland, distinctive design and highly personalized stays in the foothills of the Blue Mountains. Together, they demonstrate the range of landscapes and experiences represented within the Worlds Apart collection.</p>
<p><strong>The Curated Take:</strong> The interesting story isn’t that one collection has three MICHELIN Key hotels. It’s that the recognition doesn’t flatten them into the same idea of luxury. A historic estate, a wilderness lodge and a secluded bush retreat can all deliver exceptional hospitality while remaining deeply rooted in their individual settings. That distinction is increasingly important in Australian luxury travel, where the landscape and character of a property can be as integral to the experience as the service itself.</p>
