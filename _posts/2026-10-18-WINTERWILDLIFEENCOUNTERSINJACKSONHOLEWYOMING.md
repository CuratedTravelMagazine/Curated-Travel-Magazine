<p>Winter transforms Jackson Hole, Wyoming, into a remarkable setting for wildlife watching, as snow-covered landscapes make animal coats and tracks stand out against the white terrain. Sunrise and sunset safaris with local naturalist guides offer opportunities to observe elk, moose, bighorn sheep, and other wildlife while taking in views of the Teton Range. Snowshoeing excursions provide a closer connection to the region’s ecology, while dog sledding brings a more exhilarating way to explore the valley. At the National Elk Refuge, seasonal sleigh rides offer a memorable perspective on one of the area’s defining wildlife spectacles, as thousands of elk gather across protected winter habitat. For travelers planning a winter nature escape, Jackson Hole combines extraordinary wildlife encounters with access to Grand Teton National Park and the surrounding mountain landscape, while the Wyoming Inn offers a convenient base for exploring the region’s outdoor attractions.</p>
<h3>The Curated Take</h3>
<p>There is something extraordinary about encountering wildlife on its own terms, with a vast winter landscape creating the conditions to observe rather than simply pass through. In Jackson Hole, tracks etched into fresh snow and herds gathered across the National Elk Refuge reveal a different dimension of the American West. Guided by local experts who interpret the connections between animals and their environment, these experiences offer more than a sighting: they invite travelers to understand the rhythms of a wild landscape in winter.</p>
