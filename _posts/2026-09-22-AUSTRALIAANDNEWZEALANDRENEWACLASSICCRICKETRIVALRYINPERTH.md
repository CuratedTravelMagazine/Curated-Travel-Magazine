<p>Perth’s Optus Stadium will host the opening Test of the Chappell-Hadlee Series when Australia takes on New Zealand from December 9–13, 2026. The match marks the return of the trans-Tasman rivalry to Australian shores and will form part of the series continuing into January 2027. Premium hospitality packages will also be available, including signature dining experiences, private suites and outdoor boxes.</p>
<p>For cricket fans, the fixture offers the chance to see two neighboring nations compete in one of the sport’s most familiar rivalries, while Perth provides a distinctive setting for the opening encounter. The stadium’s hospitality options also position the event as an experience that can appeal to travelers looking to combine live sport with a more elevated spectator setting.</p>
<p><strong>The Curated Take:</strong><br/>A Test match offers a different rhythm from many major sporting events, with time to settle into the atmosphere and let the day unfold. For visitors, the Chappell-Hadlee Series provides an opportunity to experience a classic cricket rivalry while enjoying Perth’s modern stadium and broader destination appeal.</p>
