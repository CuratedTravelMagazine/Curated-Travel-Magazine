<figure><img alt="" src="https://substack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com/public/images/bff0823d-5137-4ade-841f-a9be2050462b_600x453.png"/></figure><p>Just two and a half hours from Cape Town, a different kind of South African safari experience is taking shape at Kuganha, an ultra-luxury retreat set across 10,000 hectares of rewilded land in the Cape Tankwa Karoo. Opened in March 2026 following a three-year redevelopment, the property combines private Big Five safari experiences with an unusually comprehensive approach to wellness. Its 10 Private Restoration Suites, each spanning 320 square meters, feature dedicated in-suite spa facilities, including a sauna, steam and salt therapy, cold plunge, flotation experience, outdoor pool and private relaxation spaces. A full-time wellness therapist, private safari ranger, personalized dining and dedicated concierge service further distinguish the experience. Beyond its intimate scale, Kuganha’s story is rooted in nearly three decades of conservation and habitat restoration, offering guests an opportunity to encounter South Africa’s wildlife within a landscape shaped by ecological renewal.</p><figure><img alt="" src="https://substack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com/public/images/0fa81f76-8bad-4f8c-b4ac-39ae46a44732_1200x500.jpeg"/><figcaption>Photo Credit: Kuganha, Cape Tankwa Karoo, South Africa. Courtesy of Kuganha.</figcaption></figure><h3>The Curated Take</h3><p>The next evolution of luxury safari travel may be less about how far a guest can escape and more about the quality of the experience once they arrive. Kuganha brings together two forms of restoration: the ecological renewal of the land and the personal renewal made possible by time, privacy and thoughtful wellness. Its combination of private safari, expansive tented suites and individual spa facilities creates an alternative to the conventional lodge experience, while its conservation history gives the setting a deeper sense of purpose. For travelers pairing Cape Town with a South African safari, Kuganha offers a compelling reason to venture beyond the familiar routes.</p>
