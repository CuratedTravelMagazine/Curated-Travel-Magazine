At Field & Stream Lodge Bozeman, the spirit of the outdoors meets the easygoing character of a Montana mountain town. The 179-room lodge, the debut property from Field & Stream Lodge Co., sits one mile from downtown Bozeman, offering a winter base for travelers who want access to the mountains without the pace and crowds of more heavily trafficked ski destinations. Bridger Bowl, a community-owned ski area just 20 minutes away, offers 2,000 acres of terrain, including its celebrated Ridge Terrain. Beyond downhill skiing, nearby Nordic trails and snowshoe routes provide quieter ways to experience the surrounding landscape, while local hot springs offer a welcome pause after a day outside. Back at the lodge, vintage-inspired Field & Stream details, wool camp blankets, canoe-shaped fire pits, and an indoor pool create a contemporary interpretation of the traditional outdoor lodge. The Dry Fly Saloon and Honey Hole add distinct gathering places, while an on-site adventure team connects guests with local guides, outfitters, rivers, and trails.

### The Curated Take

Not every memorable mountain escape needs to revolve around a celebrated resort or a tightly scheduled day on the slopes. Bozeman offers another kind of winter experience, where outdoor adventure remains close at hand but the rhythm of the town is part of the appeal. Field & Stream Lodge leans into that balance, bringing the nostalgia of the American outdoor lodge into a setting where a day might lead from snow-covered trails to a neighborhood gathering place, without requiring the destination itself to become the spectacle.
