<figure><img alt="" src="https://substack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com/public/images/c7bbe96c-569f-4237-b212-808e2083c9d4_5799x3866.jpeg"/></figure><p>At Chiva-Som Hua Hin, wellness is approached as a deeply personal practice, shaped as much by listening as by treatment. In Thailand’s coastal town of Hua Hin, the pioneering wellness resort begins each retreat with a private health and wellness consultation and a Lifestyle Empowerment session, bringing together nutritionists, naturopaths, therapists, and mental wellness specialists to understand each guest’s needs before developing an individualized plan. Sixteen personalized retreats and more than 200 treatments draw on Thai Traditional Medicine and contemporary wellness practices, ranging from holistic gut health therapies to Watsu, a form of water therapy in which guests are gently supported and moved through warm water. With phones kept in guest rooms, the environment encourages a deliberate step away from daily distractions, while the guidance offered throughout a stay is designed to extend beyond the resort and into everyday life.</p><figure><img alt="" src="https://substack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com/public/images/0fa81f76-8bad-4f8c-b4ac-39ae46a44732_1200x500.jpeg"/><figcaption>Photo Credit: Hua Hin, Thailand. Courtesy of Chiva-Som Hua Hin.</figcaption></figure><h3>The Curated Take</h3><p>The most meaningful measure of a wellness retreat may be what remains after departure. Chiva-Som’s emphasis on individual consultation and sustainable lifestyle changes moves the experience beyond temporary escape, placing the focus on understanding the body, cultivating healthier habits, and bringing that knowledge home. In a world where wellness is often packaged as a collection of treatments, the opportunity to be genuinely heard is a distinction worth noting.</p>
