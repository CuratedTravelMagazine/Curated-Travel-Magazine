<figure><img alt="" src="https://substack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com/public/images/ebf4a353-16ca-471e-9150-c60f85318caf_1504x1166.jpeg"/></figure><p>A beloved holiday tradition returns to the Canadian Rockies as Fairmont Jasper Park Lodge hosts Christmas in November from November 6–15, 2026. The adults-only celebration brings together acclaimed chefs, culinary demonstrations, tastings and seasonal workshops, alongside three signature evening events: the Evergreen Chalet welcome reception, Alpine Glow Dinner accompanied by The Aurora Ensemble, and the Northern Lights Ball featuring Canadian singer Nicolina Bozzo. New this year, the Fireside Reserve experience adds private culinary classes, an intimate cabin arrival, exclusive tastings and special gatherings with participating chefs and presenters. Between festivities, guests can take part in guided glacial plunges and wellness classes or simply enjoy the mountain setting and the resort’s reimagined spaces. The result is a festive escape that brings holiday spirit to the Rockies well before December.</p><figure><img alt="" src="https://substack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com/public/images/0fa81f76-8bad-4f8c-b4ac-39ae46a44732_1200x500.jpeg"/><figcaption>Photo Credit: Courtesy of Fairmont Jasper Park Lodge.</figcaption></figure><p><strong>The Curated Take</strong></p><p>Christmas in November offers a different way to experience the holiday season, trading crowded December calendars for a multi-day celebration in the Canadian Rockies. Its appeal lies in the combination of culinary discovery, festive gatherings and time to reconnect with the surrounding landscape. For travelers who enjoy making the journey itself part of the holiday ritual, Jasper provides a particularly atmospheric setting for an early-season escape.</p>
