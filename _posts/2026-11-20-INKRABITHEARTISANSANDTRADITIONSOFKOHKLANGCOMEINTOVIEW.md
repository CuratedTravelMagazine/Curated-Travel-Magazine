<figure><img alt="" src="https://substack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com/public/images/c8fe5483-4b0a-4a0b-a987-196737767af4_11144x8358.jpeg"/></figure><p>A short journey from the luxury of Phulay Bay, a Ritz-Carlton Reserve leads to Koh Klang, a traditional fishing village where local craftsmanship remains part of everyday life. Through the resort’s Krabi’s Real Touch experience, guests travel by long-tail boat through mangrove waterways before continuing by tuk-tuk through the car-free village, whose narrow lanes are not designed for conventional traffic. There, artisans demonstrate the craft of carving miniature long-tail boats from coconut shells and the intricate art of hand-painted batik. Guests are invited to try the techniques themselves, creating an opportunity to meet the makers and gain a closer appreciation for the skills behind these distinctive local traditions. The experience offers a different perspective on Krabi, extending beyond its celebrated beaches and limestone scenery to the communities and creative practices that shape southern Thailand’s cultural identity.</p><figure><img alt="" src="https://substack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com/public/images/0fa81f76-8bad-4f8c-b4ac-39ae46a44732_1200x500.jpeg"/><figcaption>Photo Credit: Koh Klang, Krabi, Thailand. Courtesy of Phulay Bay, a Ritz-Carlton Reserve.</figcaption></figure><h3>The Curated Take</h3><p>Koh Klang offers a compelling reminder that cultural discovery does not always require traveling far from a luxury resort. Sometimes it begins with a boat ride into a neighboring community and the chance to learn directly from the people who keep its traditions alive. For travelers seeking a more meaningful connection to Krabi, this experience brings the region’s living craft heritage into focus.</p>
