<figure><img alt="" src="https://substack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com/public/images/74ad037a-2a7d-462d-814b-a90dd61f6345_2560x1440.jpeg"/></figure><p>Tashkent is set to welcome a new international hotel as Minor Hotels and NH Hotels &amp; Resorts make their debut in Central Asia. NH Tashkent will have 151 rooms and a location approximately 10 minutes from the airport, with access to major venues including the UzExpo Center and Palace of International Forums. For travelers interested in the Uzbek capital’s history and everyday life, the hotel is also near two of the city’s notable landmarks: Amir Timur Square and Chorsu Bazaar. Its opening adds another international hospitality presence to a destination attracting attention for its cultural heritage.</p><figure><img alt="" src="https://substack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com/public/images/0fa81f76-8bad-4f8c-b4ac-39ae46a44732_1200x500.jpeg"/><figcaption>Photo Credit: Courtesy of Minor Hotels.</figcaption></figure><p><strong>The Curated Take</strong></p><p>Uzbekistan’s appeal reaches well beyond its role along the historic Silk Road. Tashkent offers a contemporary gateway to the country, alongside markets, architecture and cultural landmarks. The arrival of an established international hotel brand adds another option for travelers beginning or ending a wider journey through Central Asia.</p>
