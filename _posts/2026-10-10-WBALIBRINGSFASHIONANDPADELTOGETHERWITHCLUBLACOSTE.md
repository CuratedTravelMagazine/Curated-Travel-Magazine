<p>W Bali – Seminyak is bringing fashion and sport together with the arrival of Club Lacoste, marking the French brand’s longest-running Club Lacoste partnership in Asia Pacific. The collaboration introduces two padel courts alongside an official Lacoste boutique, creating a dedicated space where the fast-growing racket sport meets the brand’s fashion and lifestyle identity. The partnership also marks Lacoste’s first dedicated padel capsule, including rackets and footwear, alongside apparel, eyewear, watches, fragrances and accessories.</p>
<p>Open to both resort guests and the wider community, Club Lacoste positions padel as a social experience as much as a sport. Players can move between the courts and retail space, while W Bali’s broader setting adds dining, beachfront atmosphere and opportunities for gathering to the experience. With padel’s popularity continuing to expand internationally, the collaboration reflects how hotels are increasingly incorporating the sport into their lifestyle offerings rather than treating courts as a standalone amenity.</p>
<p><strong>The Curated Take:</strong> Club Lacoste is an interesting example of where luxury hospitality and contemporary sport are converging. Padel’s appeal is partly its accessibility and social character, making it particularly well suited to a resort environment where the experience can continue after the match. In Bali, Lacoste gets more than a court and a retail space. It gets a setting that naturally connects sport, fashion, leisure and community.</p>
