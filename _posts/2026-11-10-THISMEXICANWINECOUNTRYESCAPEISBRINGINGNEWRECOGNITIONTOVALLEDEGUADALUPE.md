<figure><img alt="" src="https://substack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com/public/images/df1d406e-fcf6-46ba-b3d2-5284eac637a4_600x450.jpeg"/></figure><p>Thirteen years after its founding, El Cielo Valle de Guadalupe continues to shape the growing appeal of Mexican wine tourism, offering travelers an immersive introduction to the vineyards, cuisine, and hospitality of Baja California. Conceived as a boutique hotel surrounded by vineyards, the destination has evolved into an integrated winery and resort where guests can explore the winemaking process through vineyard tours, cellar visits, and guided tastings. Its villa-style accommodations are set among gardens, lakes, and mountain scenery, while restaurants such as Latitud 32 and Polaris bring regional ingredients and culinary traditions to the table. The anniversary comes during a notable year for the property: El Cielo Resort received One MICHELIN Key in 2026, and the integrated winery, vineyards, and resort earned B Corp Certification for its approach to business, community, and environmental responsibility. Together, these milestones add to Valle de Guadalupe’s growing reputation as a destination for travelers seeking a more distinctive experience of Mexico, shaped by wine, food, landscape, and local culture.</p><figure><img alt="" src="https://substack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com/public/images/0fa81f76-8bad-4f8c-b4ac-39ae46a44732_1200x500.jpeg"/><figcaption>Photo Credit: El Cielo Valle de Guadalupe, Baja California, Mexico. Courtesy of El Cielo Valle de Guadalupe.</figcaption></figure><h3>The Curated Take</h3><p>Valle de Guadalupe offers a different perspective on luxury travel in Mexico, one that moves beyond the familiar coastal resort experience and into the country’s evolving culinary and wine culture. El Cielo brings together the pleasures of a vineyard stay with opportunities to understand the people, traditions, and production behind Mexican wines. Its latest recognitions are noteworthy, but the destination’s deeper appeal lies in the way it connects hospitality to the character of the region. For travelers interested in food and wine journeys with a strong sense of place, Baja California deserves a place on the itinerary.</p>
