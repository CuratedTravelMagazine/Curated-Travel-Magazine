Its 38 stilted bungalows are integrated into the rugged surroundings, while naturally flowing thermal waters, a winding thermal river and secluded wellness spaces create opportunities to slow down and reconnect with nature. Rather than imposing a conventional resort experience on the environment, the property takes its cues from the forest itself, allowing the landscape to remain central to the stay. For travelers exploring Costa Rica beyond its familiar beach destinations, Rio Perdido offers a quieter expression of the country’s natural richness, where restorative experiences and understated hospitality are closely connected to place.</p><figure><img alt="" src="https://substack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com/public/images/0fa81f76-8bad-4f8c-b4ac-39ae46a44732_1200x500.jpeg"/><figcaption>Photo Credit: Guanacaste, Costa Rica.
