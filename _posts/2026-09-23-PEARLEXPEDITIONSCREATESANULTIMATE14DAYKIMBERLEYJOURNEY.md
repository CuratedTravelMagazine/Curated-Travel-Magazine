<p>Pearl Expeditions has introduced a new 14-day 2027 itinerary combining luxury lodging, expedition cruising and aerial exploration across Western Australia’s remote Kimberley. The journey begins with two nights at adults-only El Questro Homestead before guests board the boutique expedition yacht <strong>Paspaley Pearl</strong> for ten nights along the Kimberley coast. Travelers can then add an aerial adventure, choosing between a helicopter flight over Mitchell Falls or a flight aboard Paspaley’s Grumman Mallard flying boats at Kuri Bay, before finishing in Broome.</p>
<p>The itinerary brings together several of the Kimberley’s defining experiences in a single journey, moving from a luxury wilderness lodge to small-ship cruising and finally into Australia’s pearling capital. Rather than treating the Kimberley as a quick excursion, the package is structured around allowing travelers to experience its immense coastline and remote landscapes at a slower pace.</p>
<p><strong>The Curated Take:</strong><br/>The Kimberley rewards travelers willing to make the journey, and this itinerary is built around exactly that idea. Its appeal is the variety of perspectives: wilderness from land, sea and air, with the intimacy of a small expedition vessel and the rare opportunity to experience the region from above.</p>
