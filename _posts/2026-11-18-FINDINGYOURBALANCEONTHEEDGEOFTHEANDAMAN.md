<figure><img alt="" src="https://substack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com/public/images/7fc660a0-1b2d-49b1-bbf7-8b5e58715288_2048x1366.jpeg"/></figure><p>Banyan Tree Krabi has expanded its holistic wellness offering with the opening of the Mek Khram Wellbeing Centre, a dedicated facility for Pilates, Thai boxing and other guided fitness practices at its beachfront resort in southern Thailand. Equipped with reformer and Cadillac tables, the centre offers expert-led instruction for guests ranging from Pilates beginners to those seeking a more active introduction to Thailand’s traditional martial art. The new classes complement an established programme that includes Hatha yoga, tai chi, floating meditation, forest bathing and sailing, as well as the resort’s Rainforest hydrotherapy circuit at Banyan Tree Spa. Through its Wellbeing Sanctuary programme, guests can also develop personalized itineraries based on individual goals, combining movement, meditation, nutrition, spa treatments and restorative practices. With 72 pool suites and villas set between a tropical beachfront and mountainous landscape, the resort brings together active wellness and the slower rhythms of an Andaman Sea escape.</p><figure><img alt="" src="https://substack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com/public/images/0fa81f76-8bad-4f8c-b4ac-39ae46a44732_1200x500.jpeg"/><figcaption>Photo Credit: Krabi, Thailand. Courtesy of Banyan Tree Krabi.</figcaption></figure><h3>The Curated Take</h3><p>Wellness travel has moved beyond the familiar promise of a spa treatment or a morning yoga class. At Banyan Tree Krabi, the emphasis is on creating a more integrated stay, where physical activity, cultural immersion and restorative practices can be shaped around the individual. The addition of Thai boxing is particularly fitting, introducing guests to a distinctly Thai discipline while offering a different kind of connection to the destination. For travelers seeking a luxury retreat that balances movement with genuine rest, the setting and breadth of the Wellbeing Sanctuary make wellness part of the journey rather than an amenity on the side.</p>
