<figure><img alt="" src="https://substack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com/public/images/b1f15de0-261b-4a48-8c7e-062686ac74fb_600x401.jpeg"/></figure><p>Since opening in December 2021, Raffles Bali has cultivated a distinctive expression of luxury on the hillsides above Jimbaran Bay, where 32 private pool villas are set across 23 hectares of tropical gardens overlooking the Indian Ocean. Celebrating its fifth anniversary in December 2026, the resort reflects on five years shaped by attentive butler service, cultural discovery, wellness and a deep connection to its surroundings. Guests can explore the island through botanical walks and wellness experiences, while its dining collection brings together Indonesian culinary heritage at Rumari, the immersive Rumari Counter and the coastal flavors of Peru at Loloan Coastal Peruvian. Recognized with Two MICHELIN Keys in both 2025 and 2026, Raffles Bali marks the milestone by looking ahead to its next chapter of hospitality on one of Bali’s most evocative coastlines.</p><figure><img alt="" src="https://substack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com/public/images/0fa81f76-8bad-4f8c-b4ac-39ae46a44732_1200x500.jpeg"/><figcaption>Photo Credit: Raffles Bali, Jimbaran Bay, Bali, Indonesia. Courtesy of Raffles Bali.</figcaption></figure><h3>The Curated Take</h3><p>The appeal of Raffles Bali lies in an approach to luxury that gives equal weight to privacy, place and personal connection. Its setting above Jimbaran Bay provides a sense of retreat, while its culinary and cultural experiences offer ways to engage with Bali beyond the villa. Five years in, the resort’s continued recognition suggests that gracious hospitality is not simply about beautiful surroundings, but about the thoughtful details and human encounters that make a stay memorable.</p>
