<p>More than 200 artists will spread across Western Australia’s Peel region this September for <strong>Plein Air Down Under</strong>, Australia’s largest outdoor painting festival. Taking place September 22–29, the event invites artists to paint the region’s waterways, coastline and heritage streetscapes in the open air, transforming the landscape itself into a temporary gallery. Visitors can watch artists work, attend demonstrations and participate in art activities alongside live music.</p>
<p>The festival offers an unusually intimate way to experience Mandurah/Mandjoogoordap and the wider Peel region, shifting attention from conventional sightseeing toward observation and creativity. Rather than viewing the destination through a camera lens alone, visitors can see how artists interpret the same landscapes in real time.</p>
<p><strong>The Curated Take:</strong><br/>Plein air painting encourages a slower way of seeing a destination. For travelers, the attraction isn’t only the finished art but watching artists respond to the light, water, architecture and atmosphere of the Peel in real time. It turns a regional landscape into both subject and experience.</p>
