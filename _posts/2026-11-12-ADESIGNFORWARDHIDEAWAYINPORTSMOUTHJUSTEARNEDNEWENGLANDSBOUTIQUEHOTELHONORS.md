<figure><img alt="" src="https://substack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com/public/images/81a9fc06-1e1b-4967-9b3d-bbbf0c67fff2_600x400.jpeg"/></figure><p>In the heart of downtown Portsmouth, New Hampshire, Hotel Thaxter is earning recognition for its distinctive approach to boutique hospitality, having been named New Hampshire’s Best Boutique Hotel in Boston Magazine’s 2026 New England Travel Awards. Set within a historic building just steps from Market Square, the 15-room property pairs original architectural details with contemporary interiors, bold colors and thoughtfully selected furnishings. Its residential-style suites, some with full kitchens and separate living areas, make it appealing for both weekend escapes and longer stays along New Hampshire’s Seacoast. Guests are also within easy walking distance of Portsmouth’s independent shops, galleries, theaters, restaurants and waterfront, while the hotel’s own Nichinan restaurant adds a contemporary Japanese dining experience just downstairs. The award brings fresh attention to a destination where historic character, an independent dining scene and a walkable downtown make for an appealing New England getaway.</p><figure><img alt="" src="https://substack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com/public/images/0fa81f76-8bad-4f8c-b4ac-39ae46a44732_1200x500.jpeg"/><figcaption>Photo Credit: Hotel Thaxter, Portsmouth, New Hampshire. Courtesy of Hotel Thaxter.</figcaption></figure><h3>The Curated Take</h3><p>Portsmouth has the kind of character that rewards a stay rather than a quick stop. Its historic streets, independent businesses, waterfront setting and lively culinary scene give visitors plenty of reasons to linger. Hotel Thaxter’s appeal is its connection to that atmosphere, offering a design-conscious place to return to after a day exploring the city. For travelers planning a New England escape, it is a reminder that a memorable stay does not always require a grand resort. Sometimes the right boutique hotel puts the best of a destination just outside the door.</p>
