<p>One of Perth’s established dining institutions is getting a substantial refresh. Rockpool Bar &amp; Grill at Crown Perth will temporarily close October 4 before reopening October 28 following a $3 million transformation. The fifteen-year-old restaurant will receive a redesigned bar as well as upgrades to its dining room and private spaces, while retaining the wood-fired grill, open kitchen and produce-driven menu that have defined the restaurant since its opening.</p>
<p>The renovation signals a new chapter for one of the city’s best-known premium dining venues without abandoning the elements that established its identity. For visitors staying at Crown Perth or exploring the city’s high-end restaurant scene, the refreshed restaurant will offer a familiar name in a newly designed setting.</p>
<p><strong>The Curated Take:</strong><br/>Restaurant renovations can sometimes mean a complete reinvention, but Rockpool’s approach is more about refinement. Keeping the open kitchen, wood-fired grill and Australian produce at the center while reworking the spaces suggests a refresh designed to bring the restaurant into its next era without erasing what made it recognizable.</p>
