<p>From Christmas markets and ice skating to fireworks and the legendary Hogmanay celebrations, Edinburgh, Scotland turns the winter season into an extended celebration. From November 14, 2026, through January 3, 2027, the Scottish capital will be filled with festive lights, carolers, seasonal treats and Yule logs, while the city’s Winter Festivals bring together Christmas markets, fairground rides and George Street’s Christmas Ice Rink. In East Princes Street Gardens, a traditional Christmas Market with approximately 70 stalls will sit beneath the city skyline, alongside the 46-meter-tall Big Wheel.</p>
<p>The season continues into the New Year with Edinburgh’s famous Hogmanay celebrations, including the Torchlight Procession, live music, fireworks and street food before the festivities culminate in a Street Party on Princes Street with Edinburgh Castle as its backdrop. For a stay that places travelers close to the action while offering a retreat from the crowds, The Glasshouse Hotel occupies a 160-year-old former church, transformed into a luxury boutique hotel with 77 rooms. Its two-acre rooftop garden provides panoramic views toward the city center and Calton Hill, creating a remarkably peaceful counterpoint to Edinburgh’s bustling winter calendar.</p>
<p><strong>The Curated Take:</strong> Edinburgh’s appeal during the holidays is that the city itself becomes the experience. Travelers can move from historic streets and Christmas markets to the spectacle of Hogmanay without having to manufacture a festive atmosphere around their stay. The Glasshouse adds another layer through its combination of architectural history, contemporary luxury and a private-feeling rooftop garden, making it possible to experience Edinburgh’s celebrations while still having somewhere tranquil to retreat.</p>
